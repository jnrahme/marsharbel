Saint Charbel Intercession Prayers

General Intercession Prayer

O God, infinitely holy and glorified in Your saints, You inspired Saint Charbel to live and die in perfect union with Christ. Through his intercession, grant me the grace I seek in faith and trust: [name your intention]. Help me to walk in conversion, humility, and perseverance, and to surrender completely to Your will. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.

Prayer for Healing Through Saint Charbel

O God of mercy and love, You glorified Saint Charbel by his life of holiness and prayer. Through his intercession, hear my petition. I place before You this intention: [name your intention]. If it is for Your glory and my salvation, grant healing in body, mind, and spirit. Saint Charbel, faithful servant of Christ, pray for me and lead me to trust in God. Amen.

Prayer for the Sick

Saint Charbel, compassionate intercessor for the suffering, pray for all who are ill, in pain, or under heavy trials. Ask the Lord to grant strength, patience, peace, and if it pleases Him, complete healing. May every suffering person discover Christ's nearness and hope. Amen.

Prayer for Families

Saint Charbel, protector of homes and guide of souls, intercede for our family. Obtain for us unity, forgiveness, purity of heart, and faithfulness to prayer. Guard us from division and discouragement, and lead us to live in charity and peace under God's providence. Amen.

Saint Charbel Novena Framework (9 Days)

Use this structure each day: opening prayer, day intention, one Our Father, one Hail Mary, one Glory Be, and closing prayer.

Novena Opening Prayer (Daily)

Saint Charbel, you lived hidden with Christ in God and became a light for souls through prayer, sacrifice, and Eucharistic love. I ask your intercession before the Lord for this intention: [name your intention]. Teach me faithfulness, silence, repentance, and trust in God's mercy. Amen.

Day Intentions

Day 1: Faith. Day 2: Conversion of heart. Day 3: Healing of body and soul. Day 4: Purity and discipline. Day 5: Peace in family. Day 6: Freedom from fear and anxiety. Day 7: Perseverance in prayer. Day 8: Mercy for the departed. Day 9: Thanksgiving and total surrender to God's will.

Novena Closing Prayer (Daily)

Lord Jesus Christ, through the intercession of Saint Charbel, receive my prayer and perfect Your will in me. I accept Your timing, Your wisdom, and Your mercy. May this novena bear fruit in holiness, peace, and love. Amen.

Short Daily Invocations

For Discernment

Saint Charbel, teach me obedience to God's will in every decision.

For Others

Saint Charbel, intercede for all who ask for prayer and healing.

Apostles' Creed

I believe in God, the Father almighty, Creator of Heaven and earth, and in Jesus Christ, His only Son, our Lord; who was conceived by the Holy Spirit, born of the Virgin Mary, suffered under Pontius Pilate, was crucified, died, and was buried. He descended into hell; on the third day He rose again from the dead; He ascended into Heaven, and sits at the right hand of God the Father almighty; from thence He shall come to judge the living and the dead. I believe in the Holy Spirit, the holy Catholic Church, the communion of saints, the forgiveness of sins, the resurrection of the body, and life everlasting. Amen.

Our Father

Our Father, who art in heaven, hallowed be Thy name; Thy kingdom come; Thy will be done on earth as it is in heaven. Give us this day our daily bread; and forgive us our trespasses, as we forgive those who trespass against us; and lead us not into temptation, but deliver us from evil. Amen.

Hail Mary

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

Glory Be

Glory be to the Father, and to the Son, and to the Holy Spirit; as it was in the beginning, is now, and ever shall be, world without end. Amen.

Fatima Prayer

O my Jesus, forgive us our sins, save us from the fires of hell; lead all souls to Heaven, especially those in most need of Thy mercy.

Hail Holy Queen

Hail, Holy Queen, Mother of Mercy, our life, our sweetness, and our hope. To thee do we cry, poor banished children of Eve. To thee do we send up our sighs, mourning and weeping in this valley of tears. Turn then, most gracious Advocate, thine eyes of mercy toward us; and after this, our exile, show unto us the blessed fruit of thy womb, Jesus. O clement, O loving, O sweet Virgin Mary. Pray for us, O Holy Mother of God, that we may be made worthy of the promises of Christ.

Closing Rosary Prayer

O God, whose only-begotten Son, by His life, death and resurrection, has purchased for us the rewards of eternal life; grant, we beseech Thee, that by meditating on these mysteries of the most holy Rosary of the Blessed Virgin Mary, we may imitate what they contain and obtain what they promise, through the same Christ our Lord. Amen.
