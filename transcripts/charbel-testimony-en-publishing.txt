May God bless you and sanctify you with His grace.
God's grace is your joy and your life.

Keeping vigil with Mary is not a loss; it is a gain.
She is our Mother, and the Mother of the Redeemer.

Pray, my children, pray.
The Rosary sanctifies you, and the Rosary guides you.

Mary points only to her Son Jesus.
Whoever seeks true peace should follow Mary's path to Christ.

Remember that God chose Mary.
Love her as much as you love Jesus.
Jesus entrusted her to us, and she leads us to Him.

I offer you only Jesus.
He is your nourishment,
He is the Bread and the Wine come down from heaven for the salvation of souls.

Let holy joy dwell in your hearts,
and do not let the world steal this joy from you.

Mary is with you, and her presence is not new.
Her role on earth is a call to peace,
a call to unity,
and a call to holy repentance.

Do not be afraid.
Where my Mother is, I am with her.
And I want you to be with us,
and to dwell in our heart.
Our hearts are filled with God's grace.
