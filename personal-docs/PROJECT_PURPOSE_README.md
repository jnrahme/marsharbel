# Saint Charbel Website - Purpose and Goals

## Why We Are Doing This
This project exists to build a reverent, beautiful, and practical Catholic website centered on Saint Charbel.

The main intention is not only to share information, but to help people:
- encounter God more deeply,
- understand Saint Charbel's spirituality,
- pray the Rosary with focus,
- grow in Eucharistic devotion,
- and live a concrete daily prayer life.

## Core Mission
Create a spiritually serious digital space where users can:
- learn who Saint Charbel is,
- access trustworthy devotional and historical content,
- listen to his recorded testimony,
- and be guided step-by-step into prayer (not just passive reading).

## Main Goals Right Now
1. Build a high-quality Rosary experience:
   - clear structure,
   - beginner-friendly teaching,
   - guided meditation flow,
   - scripture-inspired prompts.

2. Preserve source fidelity:
   - use uploaded source material carefully,
   - avoid inventing claims,
   - separate verified history from devotional testimony.

3. Center Saint Charbel's message:
   - prayer,
   - Christ-centered devotion,
   - Mary as path to Jesus,
   - Eucharistic life,
   - repentance and conversion.

4. Make everything accessible:
   - clean navigation,
   - mobile-friendly layout,
   - readable prayer text,
   - bilingual support where possible (Arabic + English).

5. Publish with care:
   - accurate wording,
   - respectful tone,
   - pastoral sensitivity,
   - no sensationalism.

## What Success Looks Like
A visitor can land on the site and within minutes:
- understand who Saint Charbel is,
- start praying correctly,
- follow one guided decade,
- listen to the testimony recording,
- and leave with a practical daily prayer habit.

## Current Strategic Focus
- Improve transcript quality from the recorded voice (Arabic + polished English translation).
- Keep guided prayer pages deeply meditative and easy to use.
- Organize source material without truncating essential content.
- Keep design beautiful, calm, and chapel-like.

## Non-Negotiables
- Faithful to message.
- Clear that private revelations are devotional, not dogma.
- Christ at the center of all Marian devotion.
- Encourage prayer and sacramental life, not hype.
