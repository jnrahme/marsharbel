Modern Devotional Prayer

Prayer of Mary Who Goes Ahead to Solve the Impossible

A modern devotional prayer that people keep sharing because it says something true: a Mother goes ahead of her children. You ask Mary to walk before you into the situations you cannot solve - and the prayer ends with the traditional Hail Mary, the Church's own words to her.

What This Prayer Is - and What It Is Not

This is a modern devotional prayer circulating on Catholic social media, shared by Catholic Prayer (@catholicprayerofc). It is not an official liturgical text, and the Church has not attached any promise to it. No prayer mechanically guarantees a result - including this one. What it offers is a way to ask: honestly, out loud, and through a Mother who said yes to the impossible herself.

Its closing is different: the Hail Mary that ends the prayer is the traditional prayer of the Church, prayed by Christians for centuries. One modern devotion, one ancient anchor - that combination is why people keep it.

The Prayer

Pray it slowly. Name the situation you cannot solve, and let the words carry it.

Mary, Go Before Me

Blessed Virgin Mary, Mother of God and my Mother, I come to you today with a heart that is tired and a problem that feels too big for me. You who stood at the foot of the cross and never lost faith, you who said yes to the impossible, I ask you now to go before me. Walk ahead of me into every situation that I cannot solve, into every closed door, every difficult conversation, every delay, and every fear. Prepare the way, Mother, as you prepared the wine at Cana, when no one else saw the need, and speak to your Son on my behalf. Tell Jesus what I cannot put into words. Tell him about the burden I carry, the worry that keeps me awake at night, and the situation I have been trying to control on my own. Today I let go of it, and I place it in your hands. Mary, Untier of Knots, loosen every tangle in my life, in my family, in my finances, in my health, and in my peace. Where there is no way, open a way. Where there is no hope, plant hope. Where there is division, bring reconciliation. And where there is sorrow, bring the consolation only a Mother can give.

Lord Jesus, I know that nothing is impossible for you, and that you never refuse the requests of your Mother, so through her intercession I ask you to touch my life with your mercy. Go before me, Holy Mary, and clear the path. Stay beside me, and carry me when I am weak. Remain behind me, and protect me from every harm. Holy Mother, I trust you completely. I believe that what looks impossible today will become a testimony tomorrow, and that my tears will turn into thanksgiving. Thank you, Mary, for hearing me, for walking with me, and for never leaving me alone.

The Prayer Ends with the Traditional Hail Mary

Hail Mary, full of grace, the Lord is with thee. Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb, Jesus. Holy Mary, Mother of God, pray for us sinners, now and at the hour of our death. Amen.

Where It Comes From

The prayer was shared by Catholic Prayer (@catholicprayerofc) on Instagram, where it spread as a reel. We link the original rather than re-uploading it, so the credit stays with its source.

It echoes a beloved devotion. "Mary, Untier of Knots" names a Marian devotion beloved across the world, and the wedding at Cana - where Mary saw the need before anyone else and spoke to her Son - is the Gospel scene at the prayer's heart.

Pray It Honestly

Bring the real situation

The prayer works best when you name the actual closed door - the conversation you dread, the delay you cannot move, the fear that wakes you. Vague worries make vague prayers.

Leave the outcome to God

Ask with trust, and then let go of controlling the result. What looks impossible today is God's to solve, in His way and His time - that is not a smaller promise, it is a bigger one.
