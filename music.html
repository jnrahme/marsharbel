Questions about the music

What is on this page?

Authentic Lebanese Maronite hymns to Saint Charbel, each credited to its source, with the official album or catalog linked where one exists.

Are the songs hosted on this site?

No. They are embedded from YouTube through the youtube-nocookie player, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed mode.

Where do the hymns come from?

From the Lebanese Maronite Order's official album and hymn catalog, the shrine's own channel, and recordings sung by Lebanese artists. Each track names its source.
