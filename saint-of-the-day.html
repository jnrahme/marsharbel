This week's saints

The same dated cards shown on the homepage, kept here as an archive. Dates follow the 2026 Maronite calendar; each entry links its Gospel reading and lists its sources.

  • 17.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Gregory the Wonderworker

    The Maronite calendar remembers Saint Gregory the Wonderworker on November 17. Born into a non-Christian family, Gregory encountered the Christian teacher Origen, studied with him and received baptism. He later became bishop of Neocaesarea, preaching the Gospel and guiding his community through persecution. The title Wonderworker reflects the miracles attributed to him in Christian tradition.

    Read about Saint Gregory the Wonderworker

  • 18.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Romanos the Martyr

    The Maronite calendar remembers Saint Romanos on November 18. A deacon from Caesarea in Palestine, Romanos was living in Antioch when Christians faced persecution. He encouraged the faithful to remain steadfast and publicly confessed his faith in Christ. Arrested and imprisoned, he suffered martyrdom rather than deny that faith.

    Read about Saint Romanos the Martyr

  • 19.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Pope Saint Pontian

    The Maronite calendar remembers Pope Saint Pontian on November 19. Pontian led the Church of Rome from the year 230. During persecution he was exiled to Sardinia, where he resigned his office. He died in exile in 235 and was honored in Rome as a martyr.

    Read about Pope Saint Pontian

  • 20.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Gregory of the Decapolis

    The Maronite calendar remembers Saint Gregory of the Decapolis on November 20. Drawn to prayer and the Scriptures from his youth, Gregory became a monk and traveled through the Mediterranean world. During the struggle over holy icons, he defended their veneration and encouraged Christians who faced persecution. His life is remembered for prayer, teaching and perseverance.

    Read about Saint Gregory of the Decapolis

  • 21.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Presentation of the Virgin Mary in the Temple

    The Maronite calendar celebrates the Presentation of the Virgin Mary in the Temple on November 21. The story of Mary's childhood dedication comes from early Christian tradition, not from the canonical Gospels. The feast invites Christians to contemplate Mary's gift of herself to God and her readiness to hear and follow his word.

    Read about Presentation of the Virgin Mary in the Temple

  • 22.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Announcement to the Virgin Mary

    On November 22, 2026, the Maronite calendar celebrates the Announcement to the Virgin Mary and remembers Saints Joachim and Anne, Mary's parents. In Luke's Gospel, the angel Gabriel tells Mary that she will bear Jesus, the Son of God. Mary asks how this will happen, hears that the Holy Spirit will come upon her, and freely accepts God's word. The names Joachim and Anne come from Christian tradition, not from the canonical Gospels.

    Read the Gospel account of the Announcement to Mary

Editorial Standard

Every entry names its calendar and links the sources behind it, including the Gospel reading. Where traditions differ on a saint's title or dates, the card says so instead of choosing silently. If a source corrects or withdraws an account, this page is updated to match.
