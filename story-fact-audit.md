## Verified Timeline (Date + Age)

- 1828-05-08: Birth in Bekaa Kafra (age 0).
- 1831 (reported in biographies): father dies while Charbel is very young (about age 3).
- 1851: enters Lebanese Maronite Order (age 23).
- 1853-11-01: solemn profession (age 25).
- 1859-07-23: priestly ordination (age 31).
- 1859-1875: 16 years community monastic life (age 31-47).
- 1875-1898: about 23 years hermitage life (age 47-70).
- 1898-12-24: death (age 70).
- 1965-12-05: beatification by Paul VI.
- 1977-10-09: canonization by Paul VI.
- 1993: well-known Nohad El Shami healing testimony (post-canonization devotional history).
