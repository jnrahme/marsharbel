Teaching Mode

What the Rosary is: a Christ-centered prayer with Mary, using repeated vocal prayer and Gospel meditation.

  1. Sign of the Cross
  2. Apostles' Creed
  3. Our Father
  4. 3 Hail Marys (faith, hope, charity)
  5. Glory Be
  6. Announce the mystery
  7. Our Father
  8. 10 Hail Marys with meditation
  9. Glory Be
  10. Fatima Prayer (optional)
  11. Closing prayer and Sign of the Cross

Guided Mode

Slow pace. Short lines. Gentle pauses. No pressure.

Prompts include:

  • "When you're ready..."
  • "Take a slow breath..."
  • "Hold this mystery in your heart..."

Scripture lines are paraphrased briefly for meditation.
