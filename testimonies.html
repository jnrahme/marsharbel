Source-published account

Nohad El Shami

Annaya, Lebanon - January 22, 1993

CNEWA reports that Nohad El Shami was partially paralyzed after a stroke in January 1993. Her son Saad prayed at Saint Charbel’s hermitage and brought back blessed oil and soil. According to her family, she dreamed of surgery by Saint Charbel and another monk on January 22; when she awoke, she had two neck scars. She then made a monthly pilgrimage to Annaya for the rest of her life.

Reported account - CNEWA, "Lebanon’s Beloved Saint" (interviews with the Shami family)

Source-published account

Antonio Ishaac

Lebanon - recorded at the monastery, October 2025

In an interview with CNEWA, Antonio Ishaac said he had stopped treatment for Crohn’s disease in December 2024. He visited the monastery in May 2025 and prayed; he reported that his pain vanished after an intense night. He said later tests showed the disease had disappeared. CNEWA reports that the monastery recorded his account in October 2025.

Reported account - CNEWA, "Lebanon’s Beloved Saint"

Source-published account

Mariam Assaf Awad

Lebanon - 1967 - the canonization miracle

Charbel App’s account says Mariam Assaf Awad underwent several cancer operations between 1963 and 1965. She prayed for healing through Saint Charbel’s intercession in 1967 and reported that her symptoms were gone the next morning. The Church accepted her healing as the miracle for his canonization in 1977.

Historical summary - Charbel App, Saint Charbel canonization account

Source-published account

Sister Maria Abel Kamari

Sisters of the Sacred Hearts - July 1950 - a beatification miracle

Charbel App’s account says Sister Maria Abel Kamari, bedridden with severe illness and paralysis, recovered after visiting Saint Charbel’s tomb at Annaya in July 1950. The Church approved her healing among the miracles for his beatification in 1965.

Historical summary - Charbel App, Saint Charbel canonization account

Iskandar Naim Obeid

Baabdat, Lebanon - 1950 - a beatification miracle

Charbel App’s account says Iskandar Naim Obeid, a blacksmith from Baabdat, lost sight in one eye after a metal fragment struck it. On pilgrimage to Annaya in 1950, he reported a dream of a monk touching his eye and said his sight returned. The Church approved his healing among the miracles for Saint Charbel’s beatification.

Historical summary - Charbel App, Saint Charbel canonization account
