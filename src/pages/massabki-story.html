    Read the Bible and pray together

    With a trusted adult, read Matthew 5:9 in the Gospel of Matthew. Jesus calls peacemakers children of God. Think of Francis sharing, Mooti teaching, and Raphael helping. How can you follow Jesus by making peace at home or at school?

    Then pray in your own words: Jesus, help me come closer to you, care for my neighbors, and ask a trusted adult for help when I need safety. Choose one kind act today.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who were the Massabki brothers?

    Francis, Mooti, and Raphael were three Maronite Christian laymen in Damascus, canonized with eight Franciscan friars in 2024.

    Who is this storybook for?

    Children ages 6 to 12 with a trusted adult. Pause and talk together if the sad part feels frightening.

    Does this book show violence?

    No. It tells the truth about their deaths without depicting violence. It teaches children to seek safety and adult help, and never blame a whole community for the acts of attackers.
