يا غافي وعيونك

Ya Ghafi w 3younak

Majida El Roumi sings to Saint Charbel, from her album Taranim Majidah.

Majida El Roumi, Taranim Majidah (official album). Video via YouTube (Bshara Shammas). Alternate version (St Ephrem).

زيّاح القديس شربل

Ziyyah al-Qiddis Charbel

Procession hymn, words Fr Raphael Matar, old Maronite melody (Lebanese Maronite Order hymn catalog). Video via YouTube (Sylvia Sass).

يا شربل صلّي عنّا

Ya Charbel salli aanna

Words Fr Raphael Matar, tune Fr Youssef Al-Achkar, 2008 (Lebanese Maronite Order hymn catalog). Video via YouTube (Nashat Alhaddad).

إلى قمم الروح - زيّاح مار شربل

Ila qimam al-ruh

Procession hymn to the tune "Mariam Srourak", Maronite tradition. Video via YouTube (Mario Haber).
