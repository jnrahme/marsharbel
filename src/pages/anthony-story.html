    About this story

    Illustrations are original imaginative composites, not historical footage or records of private conversations. Modern family scenes invite reflection rather than depict an event from Anthony's life.

    Ill children need rest, a trusted adult and medical care. Prayer does not replace practical help or guarantee a missing object, pet or person will be found. Missing-person danger requires adult and trained help immediately. Children are not asked to imitate hazardous travel or martyrdom.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who was Saint Anthony of Padua?

    Born Fernando in Lisbon around 1195, Anthony became a Franciscan friar, preacher and teacher. He died near Padua in 1231.

    Who is this storybook for?

    Children ages 6 to 12, reading or listening with a trusted adult. The final page has a question, Bible reading, safe action and prayer.

    Does prayer guarantee finding a lost thing?

    No. Asking Anthony to pray is a devotional practice, not a promise. Practical help matters; a missing person needs immediate adult and trained help.
