October 2026

  • Pope Leo discharged from Gemelli, back at the Vatican

    Pope Leo XIV was discharged from Gemelli Hospital on Saturday afternoon following lung surgery on Tuesday and returned to the papal apartments in Vatican City. Before leaving, he celebrated Mass in the room set aside for that purpose on the 10th floor. On Sunday he prayed the midday Angelus with thousands of faithful gathered in St Peter's Square - his first scheduled public event since being discharged - thanking the doctors, nurses and staff of Gemelli and all who prayed for him: 'I have truly felt your support.' On Wednesday, October 21, he held his first weekly General Audience since the surgery in St Peter's Square, Vatican News reported; he thanked people for their prayers and spoke on marriage and the family in his catechesis on Gaudium et Spes. RTE and ANSA also reported on the audience. Vatican News also reported that, on the evening of October 21, his doctors, Dr Luigi Carbone and Professor Stefano Margaritora, issued a statement released by the Holy See Press Office: "The final histopathology and molecular testing reports on the tissue removed during the Holy Father's surgery were issued today. The results confirmed that the tumor was a lung adenocarcinoma and that the disease was localized. A subsequent oncological assessment has confirmed that no further treatment is necessary."

    Update October 17, 2026

  • Pope Leo shows steady improvement at Gemelli

    Pope Leo XIV's doctors say October 16 blood tests and a chest X-ray confirmed steady improvement in his overall condition. Respiratory physiotherapy continues, and he can carry out normal daily activities independently. The Holy See Press Office says he celebrated Mass at noon for members of Gemelli's medical and nursing staff and the Vatican Gendarmerie, thanking those who care for the sick.

    Aoun invoked Saint Charbel's intercession for Pope Leo XIV's recovery, conveying the Lebanese people's prayers in a message to the pontiff, LBC reported. He said the pope's visit to Lebanon remains firmly rooted in the hearts and the national and spiritual consciousness of the Lebanese, regardless of their religious affiliation.

    Update October 16, 2026

  • Catholics Pray for Pope Leo at Gemelli

    An evening Rosary was held in Gemelli Hospital's chapel on October 13, presided over by Cardinal Baldassarre Reina, according to Catholic News Service reporting published by the USCCB. Churches and religious communities in Rome were also invited to join in prayer. The Holy See later reported that the Pope's operation was completed successfully.
