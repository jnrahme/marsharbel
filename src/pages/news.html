Announced December 2025 · In release rollout

"Charbel The Movie" (Mar Charbel)

Producer Nadim Mehanna (NMPRO Arts) announced the film on MTV Lebanon's talk show Sar El Wa2et on December 21, 2025, unveiling a trailer and a national fundraising campaign that raised more than $1.3 million to support an international release. Four actors portray Charbel across his life, with Jerry Ghazal as the younger Charbel and the story's central figure. The film is listed with Lebanese cinemas for 2026. This is a new production, distinct from the 2009 film "Charbel" by Nabil Lebbos.

Reported October 2, 2026 · Relics at Kfifan

Saint Charbel's Formation Monastery Receives Relics from Rome

Kfifan's Monastery of Saints Cyprian and Justina received relics of its two patron saints from Rome, MTV Lebanon reported on October 2. Mass, a blessing and a procession marked their feast. Father Emile Akiki of the Lebanese Maronite Order led the Mass. Saint Charbel studied at Kfifan before his ordination, under Saint Nimatullah Al-Hardini. The report also recalls the monastery's connection with Blessed Estephan Nehme. These are relics of Cyprian and Justina, not Saint Charbel. The ceremony's exact calendar date is not specified.

September 26, 2026 · Shrine anniversary · Reported September 30

Emmitsburg Celebrates Nine Years of Saint Sharbel's Shrine

The Family of Saint Sharbel USA reports that pilgrims gathered on September 26 at the National Shrine Grotto of Our Lady of Lourdes in Emmitsburg, Maryland, for the ninth anniversary of Saint Sharbel's shrine dedication. The celebration included Eucharistic Adoration, confessions and Mass celebrated by Father Theodore Trinko, IVE, at St. Mary's Glass Chapel. After Mass, pilgrims followed the saint's relic in procession to the shrine, where they received a blessing with Saint Sharbel's oil and venerated the relic. The organizer published its account on September 30.

September 26, 2026 · Pilgrimage

Prayer Walk from Byblos to Annaya for South Lebanon's Christians

On September 26, pilgrims walked from Byblos to Annaya carrying the intentions of the Christians who remain in Lebanon's southern border villages - Rmeich, Ain Ebel, Debel - turning the journey into an act of solidarity and hope. Believers from across Lebanon gathered in Byblos and walked some 10 miles from the Mar Girgis church to the Monastery of Saint Maron in Annaya, where the pilgrimage concluded with Mass at the Church of Saint Charbel. Organizer Anthony Jarjour of Rmeich, who walked some 112 miles from Rmeich to Annaya last year, had set out from Tyre on September 22 and reached the shrine after five days on foot - about 93 miles, rerouted for road closures. The pilgrimage was organized with the Monastery of St. Maron in Annaya and the municipalities of Byblos and Annaya, with Civil Defense and Red Cross support.

July 25, 2026 · Beatification

Maronite Patriarch Elias Hoayek Beatified at Dimane

On Saturday, July 25, 2026, Elias Hoayek, Maronite Patriarch of Antioch from 1899 to 1931, was beatified at the Patriarchal summer seat of Dimane in Lebanon. Vatican News reports that President Joseph Aoun attended, that Cardinal Marcello Semeraro, prefect of the Dicastery for the Causes of Saints, concelebrated as the envoy of Pope Leo XIV, and that Patriarch Bechara Boutros Raï gave the homily. From that day he is called Blessed. The miracle for his beatification was approved by decree on May 22, 2026. At the Angelus the next day, Pope Leo XIV called him "a man of dialogue and hope" and the "Father of Greater Lebanon." Hoayek was born in Helta on December 4, 1843, founded the Maronite Sisters of the Holy Family, and died at Bkerke on December 24, 1931. He decided to build the shrine at Harissa.

September 27, 2026 · Beatification

Three Priests Martyred at Monte Sole Beatified in Bologna

On Sunday, September 27, in the Basilica of San Petronio in Bologna, Cardinal Marcello Semeraro, prefect of the Dicastery for the Causes of Saints, beatified three priests killed by Nazi forces in the 1944 Monte Sole massacres: Don Ubaldo Marchioni, a diocesan priest; Don Elia Comini, a Salesian; and Father Martino Capelli, a Dehonian. Because the Church has recognized them as martyrs, no miracle was required for their beatification - from Sunday they are called Blessed. Bologna Today reported that a bullet-pierced pyx, found in the rubble of the altar of Casaglia's church, has become a symbol of the massacre the three priests did not escape.

September 24, 2026 · Beatification

Fulton Sheen Beatified in Saint Louis Before 50,000

Archbishop Fulton J. Sheen - the Emmy-winning broadcaster of Life Is Worth Living - was declared Blessed at a Mass in The Dome at America's Center in Saint Louis, Missouri. Cardinal Luis Antonio Tagle presided for Pope Leo XIV before a crowd organizers estimated near 50,000; the miracle that cleared the way was the 2010 revival of James Fulton Engstrom, who carried Sheen's relic in the procession. A day earlier, Pope Leo XIV praised Sheen's media apostolate at the Wednesday General Audience. He is Blessed, not yet a saint - his cause for canonization continues.
