Rosary Prayer Coach

The Rosary Prayer Coach builds a calm guided Rosary session around the time you have and the intention you bring. Choose five, ten, or twenty minutes, pick a mystery set, and it leads you through each prayer slowly, with short lines, gentle pauses, and no pressure.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does the Rosary Prayer Coach do?

It builds a guided Rosary session around the time you have and the intention you bring, then leads you through each prayer step by step, at a slow pace, with short lines and gentle pauses.

How long can a session be?

Five, ten, or twenty minutes - you choose at the start, and the coach shapes the session to fit.

What is guided mode like?

Slow and unhurried: short lines, gentle pauses, and prompts like "When you're ready" and "Take a slow breath." Scripture lines are paraphrased briefly for meditation.

What is the shape of the prayers in a session?

The Sign of the Cross, Apostles' Creed, Our Father, three Hail Marys, and Glory Be to open; then for each decade the mystery is announced, one Our Father and ten Hail Marys with meditation, the Glory Be, and an optional Fatima prayer; a closing prayer and the Sign of the Cross end the session.
