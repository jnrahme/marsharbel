Charbel Premieres in Beirut; Lebanese Cinema Release Set for October 22

Charbel, the new feature film about Saint Charbel, held its special premiere on October 9 at BlackBox in Beirut Media Zone, according to Nidaa al-Watan's October 10 report. The film is a joint NMPRO and MTV production, directed by Nadim Mehanna and written by Claudia Marshalian, with Jerry Ghazal playing the young Charbel and Father Roukoz El Hajj portraying him later in life. Cinemacity lists a runtime of 130 minutes.

Lebtalks reported that the October 9 launch was under President Joseph Aoun's patronage and would be broadcast on MTV, with a nationwide Lebanese theatrical rollout planned from October 22. Cinemacity Beirut independently lists Thursday, October 22 as its opening date. Its page still has no screening sessions scheduled. The completed premiere and the upcoming public cinema opening are different milestones.

A separate charity screening in support of Anta Akhi is scheduled for October 12 at ABC Achrafieh. International release dates, streaming distribution and English-language availability remain unconfirmed. L'Orient-Le Jour: October 12 charity screening for Anta Akhi (October 8, French)
