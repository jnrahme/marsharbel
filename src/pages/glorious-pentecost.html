    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the Third Glorious Mystery?

    The Descent of the Holy Spirit. The apostles were gathered with Mary when a sound like a mighty wind filled the house, and tongues of fire rested on each of them. This mystery is traditionally prayed on Wednesday / Sunday, and its fruit is wisdom and apostolic boldness.

    How do you pray this decade with children?

    Read one page at a time. On the decade page, pray the Our Father once, the Hail Mary ten times (one for each bead), then the Glory Be and the Fatima prayer.
