Words Reported Through Others

Some of the most repeated "Saint Charbel quotes" were not written by him at all - they are words others reported hearing from him, and they belong to testimony, not to his writings. The most famous comes from Nohad El Shami, the Lebanese mother of twelve whose healing in January 1993 began the 22nd-of-the-month pilgrimage: she recounted that Saint Charbel came to her in a dream and told her, "I have come to operate on you." That sentence is her testimony - treasured by the monastery and retold everywhere - and we present it as such, not as a written saying of the saint.

The same is true of his death. Biographies record that he died on Christmas Eve 1898 while celebrating Mass, having been struck by illness days earlier at the altar, and they hand down his last recorded offering - that he had given everything to the monastery and now gave his soul to Christ. These are the community's remembered words about his death, not sentences from his pen.

How to Spot a Misattributed Quote

Search the internet for Saint Charbel quotes and you will find long lists - many beautiful, most unsourced, some written by other saints or by modern authors. Before sharing one, ask two questions:

  • Does it appear in a traceable collection? {{copy quotes-attribution checkCollection}}
