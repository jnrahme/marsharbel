The Story of Saint Joseph

Joseph cared for Mary and Jesus through journeys, hard decisions and the ordinary work of family life.

Short answer: This ten-page Saint Joseph storybook for ages 6 to 12 follows the Gospel accounts of Joseph welcoming Mary, traveling to Bethlehem, seeking safety in Egypt, returning to Nazareth and finding Jesus in Jerusalem. The final page offers a question, a Bible reading, a safe action and a prayer.

    Read the Bible and pray together

    With a trusted adult, read Luke 2:51-52. Jesus returned to Nazareth and grew in his family. Talk about one small, safe way to help at home, with room for rest and play.

    About this story

    Illustrations are original imaginative composites, not records of private conversations, exact rooms or workshop routines. Gospel events are attributed to Matthew or Luke. Modern reflection scenes are not historical footage.

    Children are not responsible for keeping a family safe, traveling alone or solving a missing-person emergency. Seek a safe place and trusted adult when threatened or separated. Ordinary help must fit the child's age, with room for rest and play. No dream warning or rescue is promised to readers.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who was Saint Joseph?

    Joseph was the carpenter of Nazareth who cared for Mary and Jesus. Matthew and Luke describe his decisions and journeys.

    Who is this storybook for?

    Children ages 6 to 12, reading or listening with a trusted adult. The final page has a question, Bible reading, safe action and prayer.

    Are the pictures exact historical scenes?

    No. The Gospel events are sourced; rooms, workshop details and visual impressions are imaginative composites, not eyewitness records.
