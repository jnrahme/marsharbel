The Agony in the Garden: First Sorrowful Mystery

A little read-aloud book for one decade of the Rosary: The Agony in the Garden.

Short answer: This book walks children through the First Sorrowful Mystery, the Agony in the Garden: a short story, the Gospel passage, the Our Father, ten Hail Marys and the Glory Be, and a reflection on conformity to the Father's will.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the First Sorrowful Mystery?

    The Agony in the Garden. In the garden of Gethsemane, Jesus prayed while his friends slept. This mystery is traditionally prayed on Tuesday / Friday, and its fruit is conformity to the Father's will.

    How do you pray this decade with children?

    Read one page at a time. On the decade page, pray the Our Father once, the Hail Mary ten times (one for each bead), then the Glory Be and the Fatima prayer.
