His Last Days

How Did Saint Charbel Die?

Saint Charbel died on the night of December 24, 1898 - Christmas Eve - at the monastery of Annaya, aged seventy. Eight days earlier, on December 16, he had been struck by a sudden illness, recorded as a stroke, while celebrating Mass. He spent his last days as he had lived: in prayer, without complaint.

The Last Illness

On December 16, 1898, during the celebration of Mass, Saint Charbel was struck by a sudden illness. The Vatican's account records it simply: struck down at the altar, he died eight days later. He was seventy years old, and had spent twenty-three of them as a hermit at the hermitage of Saints Peter and Paul above the monastery - a body long thinned by fasting and austerity, which those around him believed had simply worn out in God's service.

His Final Mass

The Mass of December 16 was his last. The tradition holds that he was struck during the liturgy itself - the daily Mass that had been the center of his life for nearly forty years as a priest. It is the detail every account returns to: the monk who had asked for nothing but God was taken while doing the thing he loved most, at the altar.

Death on Christmas Eve

He lingered eight days. On the night of December 24, 1898 - Christmas Eve - he died at Annaya. The Vatican's Saint of the Day entry says he ended his life "as he had lived it: united with Christ in the Eucharist." He had no possessions to leave behind; a hermit owns nothing worth inheriting.

A Funeral Through the Snow

He was buried in the monastery cemetery at Annaya, in the depth of the Lebanese mountain winter. The tradition remembers monks and villagers carrying the hermit to his grave through the snow - the same mountains he had walked barefoot for decades. It was a monk's funeral: no monument, no marble, a name among brothers.

What Happened Next

The grave was not the end of the story. Within days, villagers were reporting lights over the tomb; within months, the Church opened it and found the body fresh. The exhumations, the physicians' testimony, and the tomb as it stands today - visited by Pope Leo XIV in December 2025 - are on our tomb and incorrupt body page. He was beatified on December 5, 1965, and canonized on October 9, 1977.

Frequently Asked Questions

When did Saint Charbel die?

On the night of December 24, 1898 - Christmas Eve - at the monastery of Annaya in Lebanon, aged seventy.

What did Saint Charbel die of?

A sudden illness recorded as a stroke, which struck him during Mass on December 16, 1898. He died eight days later.

Where did Saint Charbel die?

At the monastery of Saint Maron in Annaya, in the Jbeil (Byblos) district of North Lebanon - the monastery below the hermitage where he had lived for twenty-three years.

How old was Saint Charbel when he died?

Seventy. He was born on May 8, 1828, in Bekaa Kafra, and died on December 24, 1898.

What happened to Saint Charbel's body after he died?

He was buried in the monastery cemetery; reported lights over the grave led the Church to open the tomb in 1899, and the body was found remarkably preserved. The full account, with the physicians' testimony, is on the tomb page.
