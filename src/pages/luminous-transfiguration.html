The Transfiguration: Fourth Luminous Mystery

A little read-aloud book for one decade of the Rosary: The Transfiguration.

Short answer: This book walks children through the Fourth Luminous Mystery, the Transfiguration: a short story, the Gospel passage, the Our Father, ten Hail Marys and the Glory Be, and a reflection on desire for holiness and spiritual courage.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is the Fourth Luminous Mystery?

    The Transfiguration. On a high mountain, Jesus' face shone like the sun and his clothes became white as snow, while Moses and Elijah talked with him. This mystery is traditionally prayed on Thursday, and its fruit is desire for holiness and spiritual courage.

    How do you pray this decade with children?

    Read one page at a time. On the decade page, pray the Our Father once, the Hail Mary ten times (one for each bead), then the Glory Be and the Fatima prayer.
