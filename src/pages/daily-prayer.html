Daily Prayer

Begin Every Morning in Prayer

One short email at 6 AM: a prayer and the day's rosary to pray or meditate with, the Maronite prayers of the day, and a prayer to Saint Charbel. Free, and you can stop it with one tap.

What arrives each morning

A prayer to open the day

A short morning prayer you can pray in a quiet minute, or carry with you through the day.

The day's rosary

The mystery set for the day, listed one by one - ready to pray or to meditate at your own pace.

A prayer to Saint Charbel

A daily prayer asking his intercession, with a link to all the Maronite prayers of the day.

From the daily email

O God, infinitely holy and glorified in Your saints, You inspired Saint Charbel to live and die in perfect union with Christ. Through his intercession, grant me the grace I seek in faith and trust: [name your intention]. Help me to walk in conversion, humility, and perseverance, and to surrender completely to Your will. Through Christ our Lord. Amen.
