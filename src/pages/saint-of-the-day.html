This week's saints

The same dated cards shown on the homepage, kept here as an archive. Dates follow the 2026 Maronite calendar; each entry links its Gospel reading and lists its sources.

  • 17.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Gregory the Wonderworker

    The Maronite calendar remembers Saint Gregory the Wonderworker on November 17. Born into a non-Christian family, Gregory encountered the Christian teacher Origen, studied with him and received baptism. He later became bishop of Neocaesarea, preaching the Gospel and guiding his community through persecution. The title Wonderworker reflects the miracles attributed to him in Christian tradition.

    Read about Saint Gregory the Wonderworker

  • 18.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Romanos the Martyr

    The Maronite calendar remembers Saint Romanos on November 18. A deacon from Caesarea in Palestine, Romanos was living in Antioch when Christians faced persecution. He encouraged the faithful to remain steadfast and publicly confessed his faith in Christ. Arrested and imprisoned, he suffered martyrdom rather than deny that faith.

    Read about Saint Romanos the Martyr

  • 19.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Pope Saint Pontian

    The Maronite calendar remembers Pope Saint Pontian on November 19. Pontian led the Church of Rome from the year 230. During persecution he was exiled to Sardinia, where he resigned his office. He died in exile in 235 and was honored in Rome as a martyr.

    Read about Pope Saint Pontian

  • 20.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Gregory of the Decapolis

    The Maronite calendar remembers Saint Gregory of the Decapolis on November 20. Drawn to prayer and the Scriptures from his youth, Gregory became a monk and traveled through the Mediterranean world. During the struggle over holy icons, he defended their veneration and encouraged Christians who faced persecution. His life is remembered for prayer, teaching and perseverance.

    Read about Saint Gregory of the Decapolis

  • 21.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Presentation of the Virgin Mary in the Temple

    The Maronite calendar celebrates the Presentation of the Virgin Mary in the Temple on November 21. The story of Mary's childhood dedication comes from early Christian tradition, not from the canonical Gospels. The feast invites Christians to contemplate Mary's gift of herself to God and her readiness to hear and follow his word.

    Read about Presentation of the Virgin Mary in the Temple

  • 22.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Announcement to the Virgin Mary

    On November 22, 2026, the Maronite calendar celebrates the Announcement to the Virgin Mary and remembers Saints Joachim and Anne, Mary's parents. In Luke's Gospel, the angel Gabriel tells Mary that she will bear Jesus, the Son of God. Mary asks how this will happen, hears that the Holy Spirit will come upon her, and freely accepts God's word. The names Joachim and Anne come from Christian tradition, not from the canonical Gospels.

    Read the Gospel account of the Announcement to Mary

  • 23.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Sassine, Bishop of Cyzicus

    On November 23, 2026, the Maronite calendar remembers Saint Sassine, Bishop of Cyzicus, honored as a martyr. Cyzicus was a city on the Sea of Marmara in what is now Turkey. During the persecution of the emperor Diocletian, Sassine was tortured for confessing Christ; one early account says he was tied behind a horse to be dragged through the streets until death. Sassine is the Maronite rendering of the name Sisinnius.

    Identity and witness: Orthodox Church in America synaxarion entry for Sisinius of Cyzicus, commemorated November 23, and the Maronite Synaxarion of the Antiochian Maronite Rite (Kaslik, 1996). The date and the naming Bishop of Cyzicus, Martyr, follow the 2026 Eparchy of St. Maron liturgical calendar.

    Read the Gospel proclaimed on Saint Sassine's day

  • 24.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Catherine of Alexandria

    On November 24, the Maronite calendar remembers Saint Catherine of Alexandria, martyr. Later tradition portrays her as a learned young Christian who defended her faith before an emperor and whose witness led philosophers to Christ. Remembered for remaining faithful even unto death, she invites us to seek wisdom and courage in Jesus.

    Brief account based on the Catholic Encyclopedia and Franciscan Media, distinguishing later legend from established history. The date follows the 2026 Maronite calendar; the Roman calendar remembers Catherine on November 25.

    Read about Saint Catherine of Alexandria

  • 25.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Pope Saint Clement I

    On November 25, the Maronite calendar remembers Pope Saint Clement I, an early bishop of Rome. In his letter to the Christians of Corinth, he calls them away from quarrels and toward repentance, humility and peace. His words invite us to seek mercy in Christ and to forgive one another.

    Original summary of Clement’s letter to the Corinthians, published by the Vatican. The date follows the 2026 Maronite calendar; the Roman calendar remembers Clement on November 23.

    Read Saint Clement’s letter

  • 26.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Peter of Alexandria

    On November 26, the Maronite calendar remembers Saint Peter of Alexandria, bishop and martyr. He led the Church of Alexandria during persecution and died as a martyr in 311. His witness invites us to remain faithful to Christ in hardship.

    Brief account based on the Catholic Encyclopedia. The date follows the 2026 Maronite calendar.

    Read about Saint Peter of Alexandria

  • 27.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint James Intercisus

    According to his traditional life, James was a Christian at the Persian court who turned away from his faith, then repented after an appeal from his family. He later confessed Christ and was killed for his faith. His memory invites us to return to God and begin again.

    Summary of the traditional life published by the Orthodox Church in America. The date follows the 2026 Maronite calendar.

    Learn more

  • 28.11.2026 Maronite calendar

    Saint Stephen the Younger

    Stephen was a monk remembered for defending the veneration of holy icons during the eighth-century controversy. His traditional life tells of imprisonment and of encouraging fellow prisoners in prayer. He is honored as a martyr.

    Summary of the traditional life published by the Orthodox Church in America. The date follows the 2026 Maronite calendar.
