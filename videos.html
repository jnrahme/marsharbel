A Note on These Videos

Every video here is hosted on YouTube by the channel that made it - we did not produce them and cannot vouch for every word in every report. Where a video tells a miracle story, our own pages keep the careful distinctions: what the Church has formally recognized, what is recorded at the monastery, and what remains a personal testimony. What videos are on this page?

No. They are embedded from YouTube through the youtube-nocookie player, using YouTube's privacy-enhanced embed mode.
