A young saint’s invitation

Saint Carlo Acutis helped create an international exhibition of Eucharistic miracles. This independent collection continues his invitation to pay attention to the Eucharist, with original writing and direct links to the sanctuaries, dioceses and historical records behind each account. How to read these accounts

A tradition is a tradition. A diocesan investigation is not the same as a universal declaration. Scientific observations do not establish a supernatural cause. Each page names its source and distinguishes what was reported from what can be independently concluded. Our opening five are a beginning, not every miracle in the world.
