A young saint’s invitation

Saint Carlo Acutis helped create an international exhibition of Eucharistic miracles. This independent collection continues his invitation to pay attention to the Eucharist, with original writing and direct links to the sanctuaries, dioceses and historical records behind each account. Carlo was canonized on September 7, 2025. About Carlo’s exhibition · Vatican canonization record

How to read these accounts

A tradition is a tradition. A diocesan investigation is not the same as a universal declaration. Scientific observations do not establish a supernatural cause. Each page names its source and distinguishes what was reported from what can be independently concluded. This growing collection is not every reported miracle in the world.
