A modern inquiry · Sokółka, Poland · 2008-09

Sokółka: A Commission’s Careful Account

In Poland, a diocesan commission recorded what it examined, and what it did not claim to prove.

01

What happened in church

The Białystok Metropolitan Curia says that on October 12, 2008, a consecrated host fell during Communion. It was placed in water. On October 19, staff saw a red mark on it. The curia set up a commission in March 2009 to review witnesses and specialists’ findings.

02

A measured finding

The curia reports that two independent pathomorphologists found the sample resembled heart muscle tissue more than other living tissues. Their wording says “indicates” or “most resembles,” not that they established a supernatural cause. The commission said it found no outside interference and forwarded its records to the Apostolic Nunciature.

03

What the statement concludes

The curia concluded that the event did not contradict the Church’s faith and called for reverence toward the Eucharist. This site does not upgrade that statement into a claim of Vatican certification or independent scientific proof of a miracle.

Pause and reflect

The commission’s care is part of this story. Reverence need not make us less truthful; it can make us more attentive to what is known and what remains a mystery.
