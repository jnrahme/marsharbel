A recovered treasure · Siena, Italy · 1730

Siena: The Hosts Kept Across Centuries

A theft, a recovery, and hosts long guarded by the people of Siena.

01

August 1730

According to the Archdiocese of Siena, thieves took a ciborium containing 351 consecrated hosts from the Basilica of San Francesco on August 14. The hosts were found on August 17 in the nearby sanctuary of Santa Maria in Provenzano and returned to San Francesco in procession.

02

What is preserved

In its 2014 account, the archdiocese said 223 hosts remained and are venerated in the basilica. It describes repeated examinations and quotes a 1914 commission on their unusual preservation. The numbers, tests and devotional interpretation are those reported by the archdiocese, not an independent modern verification by this site.

03

More than an anomaly

In Siena the story also carries a memory of loss and recovery: people sought what had been taken and kept watch over what came back. The archdiocese records the continuing devotion around the hosts and the annual remembrance of their discovery.

Pause and reflect

To guard something sacred is also to give it attention. Consider one small act of reverence you can offer this week.
