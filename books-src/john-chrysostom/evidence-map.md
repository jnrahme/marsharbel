# St. John Chrysostom — claim-to-source map (v1)
Primary working source: the site's own researched page st-john-chrysostom.html (origin/stage), citing Benedict XVI's two general audiences (Sept 19 & 26, 2007), the public-domain homilies (New Advent) and Palladius' Dialogue.

| Claim | Page | Source status | Allowed wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Born Antioch c. 349; widowed mother Anthusa raised him in the faith | 1 | documented (Benedict XVI audience via site) | as written |
| Studied rhetoric under Libanius; turned from a brilliant legal career to the Church; baptized; six years as a monk (4 under an ascetic elder, 2 alone in a cave memorizing Scripture); health broke | 2 | documented | as written |
| Ordained priest 386; preached 12 years at Antioch's great church; homilies walk Matthew, John, Genesis, Paul verse by verse into daily life; name Chrysostomos ("golden-mouthed") given by later generations | 3 | documented | as written |
| 387 tax riot; sermons On the Statues held the city steady through Lent until the emperor's pardon | 4 | documented | as written |
| 398 taken practically against his will to be archbishop of Constantinople; preached that Christ is hungry in the man at the door; heard at court as an attack | 5 | documented | as written |
| Cut episcopal household spending to fund hospitals and the poor; deposed at Synod of the Oak 403; city erupted; recalled within days; exiled for good June 404 (Cucusus); exile letters circulated like homilies | 6 | documented | as written |
| Quote: "Would you honor the body of Christ? Do not neglect him when he is naked." + "This is my body" / "I was hungry and you gave me no food" connection | 7 | traceable text (Homily 50 on Matthew) | verbatim |
| Died on forced march at Comana in Pontus, Sept 14, 407; last recorded words "Glory to God for all things" per his disciple Palladius | 8 | documented (Palladius) | verbatim quote; plain "died" |
| 438: Theodosius II (son of the empress who exiled him) returned his relics to Constantinople in triumph and publicly asked forgiveness | 9 | documented | as written |
| Liturgy of St John Chrysostom = ordinary Sunday liturgy of Byzantine churches, Catholic and Orthodox; Antioch = mother see of the Maronite Church | 9 | documented | as written |
