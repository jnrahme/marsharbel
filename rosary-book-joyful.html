Opening

"And in the sixth month, the angel Gabriel was sent from God into a city of Galilee, called Nazareth, To a virgin espoused to a man whose name was Joseph, of the house of David; and the virgin's name was Mary."

Luke 1:26-27

In the first century, in Galilee, in a town called Nazareth, God sent an angel to a young woman named Mary. The Rosary follows her Son's life, one mystery at a time, in four sets of five. Begin with Luke.

The Joyful Mysteries

The Annunciation

"Behold the handmaid of the Lord; be it done to me according to thy word."

Luke 1:38

The angel says, "Hail, full of grace, the Lord is with thee: blessed art thou among women." Mary is greatly troubled, and she thinks about what such a greeting could mean. "Fear not, Mary, for thou hast found grace with God." She asks her question. Then she says yes.

Luke 1:28-30, 1:38

The Joyful Mysteries

The Visitation

"Blessed art thou among women, and blessed is the fruit of thy womb."

Luke 1:42

Mary goes into the hill country with haste. She enters Zechariah's house and greets Elizabeth. Elizabeth cries out in blessing, and Mary answers with a song: "My soul doth magnify the Lord. And my spirit hath rejoiced in God my Saviour." She sings before anything has been fixed.

Luke 1:39-47

The Joyful Mysteries

The Nativity

"And she brought forth her firstborn son, and wrapped him up in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger; because there was no room for them in the inn."

Luke 2:7

"...wrapped him up in swaddling clothes, and laid him in a manger; because there was no room for them in the inn." Shepherds hurry to find "Mary and Joseph, and the infant lying in the manger." An angel has told them: "...a Saviour, who is Christ the Lord..."

Luke 2:7, 2:11, 2:16

The Joyful Mysteries

The Presentation

"And thy own soul a sword shall pierce, that, out of many hearts, thoughts may be revealed."

Luke 2:35

In the Temple, Simeon takes the child in his arms. "Because my eyes have seen thy salvation," he says. Then he turns to Mary and tells her something heavy and true. Then Anna, a prophet who has not left the Temple, comes up at that very hour and gives thanks to the Lord.

Luke 2:29-38

The Joyful Mysteries

The Finding in the Temple

"How is it that you sought me? did you not know, that I must be about my father's business?"

Luke 2:49

After three days they find him in the Temple, sitting among the teachers, listening and asking questions. His mother says, "Son, why hast thou done so to us? behold thy father and I have sought thee sorrowing." He goes down with them to Nazareth, and "...his mother kept all these words in her heart."

Luke 2:46-51
