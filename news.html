Latest news

Dated, sourced reports. Reported events and newly recorded cases are not the same as formal Vatican recognition; the label on each report says which is which.

October 2026

  • Pope Leo's recovery is progressing normally

    The Holy See says Pope Leo XIV's scheduled blood tests and chest X-ray confirm that recovery is progressing normally after his lung surgery. He has begun respiratory physiotherapy and walking in his room, and has resumed eating normally.

    In a message to the pontiff, Lebanese President Joseph Aoun wished Pope Leo XIV a speedy recovery, conveying the sentiments and prayers of the Lebanese people and invoking the intercession of Saint Charbel, whose shrine the pope visited, LBC reported. Aoun said the pope's visit to Lebanon remains firmly rooted in the hearts and the national and spiritual consciousness of the Lebanese, regardless of their religious affiliation.

    Update October 14, 2026

  • Catholics Pray for Pope Leo at Gemelli

    An evening Rosary was held in Gemelli Hospital's chapel on October 13, presided over by Cardinal Baldassarre Reina, according to Catholic News Service reporting published by the USCCB. Churches and religious communities in Rome were also invited to join in prayer. The Holy See later reported that the Pope's operation was completed successfully.

    Prayer October 13, 2026

  • Charbel Premieres in Beirut

    The NMPRO and MTV feature premiered October 9 at Beirut Media Zone; the Lebanese cinema release is planned from October 22. International release and streaming remain unconfirmed.

  • Beirut Cathedral Consecrates an Altar to Blessed Patriarchs Douaihy and Hoayek

    Cardinal Al-Rahi consecrated an altar to Blessed Patriarchs Douaihy and Hoayek at Beirut's Saint George Cathedral. Both remain Blessed, not canonized saints.

    Event September 27, 2026

  • Three Priests Martyred at Monte Sole Beatified in Bologna

    Cardinal Semeraro beatified three priests killed in the 1944 Monte Sole massacres; recognized as martyrs, no miracle was required.

    Pilgrims walked some 10 miles from Byblos to the Monastery of Saint Maron in Annaya, carrying the intentions of Christians in the southern border villages.
