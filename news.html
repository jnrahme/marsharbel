October 2026

  • Minya cathedral welcomes relics of Saint Charbel and Blessed Margherita Caiani

    Bishop Basilios Fawzy, Coptic Catholic Metropolitan of Minya, presided over the Divine Liturgy at Christ the King Cathedral in Minya on Sunday welcoming the relics of Saint Charbel and Blessed Margherita Caiani, founder of the Franciscan Little Sisters of the Sacred Heart, according to Egyptian press reports. In a homily titled "A Call to Holiness," he described the presence of the saints' relics as a divine gift to the parish and an encouragement to imitate their lives, noting that holiness is possible for whoever responds to God's grace. Fathers Kyrillos Maximos and Youhanna Samuel, pastors of the cathedral, and Father Andrawos Farag, pastor of St Mark's Church in Sydney, Australia, joined the celebration.

    Update October 25, 2026

  • Al-Rahi celebrates Mass in Al-Aqoura for first centenary of Massabki brothers' beatification

    Maronite Patriarch Bechara al-Rahi celebrated the Divine Liturgy in Al-Aqoura on Sunday for the first centenary of the beatification of the Massabki brothers. "The Massabki gave their lives for Christ, and their family traces its roots to the town of Al-Aqoura, and this town today takes them back and takes back their memory," he said, adding that "their holiness did not begin at the hour of martyrdom but appeared in their faith, their prayer and their love of doing good." Beatified by Pope Pius XI on October 10, 1926, the brothers Francis, Abdel Moati and Raphael were canonized by Pope Francis on October 20, 2024, the first Maronite laymen to be canonized; they were martyred in Damascus on the night of July 9-10, 1860, and belonged to the Maronite Eparchy of Damascus. Al-Aqoura mayor Boutros Mehanna had announced that the Patriarch would preside at Mar Girgis Cathedral, describing it as a religious and national celebration affirming the holiness of the town, region and Lebanon.

    Update October 25, 2026

  • Pope Leo XIV cancels Assisi peace-meeting visit on doctors' advice

    The Holy See Press Office announced on Friday that Pope Leo XIV will not take part in the "Unarmed and Disarming Peace" meeting in Assisi on October 27. His doctors, Dr Luigi Carbone and Professor Stefano Margaritora, advised against the planned helicopter journey so soon after his October 13 thoracic surgery, and a scheduled follow-up chest X-ray confirmed that his postoperative recovery is progressing very well. The Pope will send a message expressing his closeness to the organizers, religious leaders and guests taking part. The St. Egidio Community meeting (October 25-27) marks the 40th anniversary of the World Day of Prayer for Peace convened by Saint John Paul II in Assisi in 1986, bringing together 384 representatives from 53 countries. Reuters reports the Vatican has not said whether the Pope's scheduled November 6-17 trip to Uruguay, Argentina and Peru will go ahead.

    Update October 23, 2026

  • Vatican Doctrinal Dicastery Publishes 'Venite ad Me' on Handing On the Faith

    The Dicastery for the Doctrine of the Faith published 'Venite ad Me: Considerations on the Transmission of the Faith in an Epochal Change' on October 22. The Dicastery presents it as a set of Considerations, not a doctrinal note, calling for a renewed proclamation of the Gospel and new paths for evangelizing adults as well as catechizing children. Vatican News reports Pope Leo XIV approved the document on August 21, 2026.

    Announcement October 22, 2026

  • Pope Leo discharged from Gemelli, back at the Vatican

    Pope Leo XIV was discharged from Gemelli Hospital on Saturday afternoon following lung surgery on Tuesday and returned to the papal apartments in Vatican City. Before leaving, he celebrated Mass in the room set aside for that purpose on the 10th floor. On Sunday he prayed the midday Angelus with thousands of faithful gathered in St Peter's Square - his first scheduled public event since being discharged - thanking the doctors, nurses and staff of Gemelli and all who prayed for him: 'I have truly felt your support.' On Wednesday, October 21, he held his first weekly General Audience since the surgery in St Peter's Square, Vatican News reported; he thanked people for their prayers and spoke on marriage and the family in his catechesis on Gaudium et Spes. RTE and ANSA also reported on the audience. Vatican News also reported that, on the evening of October 21, his doctors, Dr Luigi Carbone and Professor Stefano Margaritora, issued a statement released by the Holy See Press Office: "The final histopathology and molecular testing reports on the tissue removed during the Holy Father's surgery were issued today. The results confirmed that the tumor was a lung adenocarcinoma and that the disease was localized. A subsequent oncological assessment has confirmed that no further treatment is necessary."

    Update October 17, 2026

  • Pope Leo shows steady improvement at Gemelli

    Pope Leo XIV's doctors say October 16 blood tests and a chest X-ray confirmed steady improvement in his overall condition. Respiratory physiotherapy continues, and he can carry out normal daily activities independently. The Holy See Press Office says he celebrated Mass at noon for members of Gemelli's medical and nursing staff and the Vatican Gendarmerie, thanking those who care for the sick.

    Aoun invoked Saint Charbel's intercession for Pope Leo XIV's recovery, conveying the Lebanese people's prayers in a message to the pontiff, LBC reported. He said the pope's visit to Lebanon remains firmly rooted in the hearts and the national and spiritual consciousness of the Lebanese, regardless of their religious affiliation.

    Update October 16, 2026

  • Catholics Pray for Pope Leo at Gemelli

    An evening Rosary was held in Gemelli Hospital's chapel on October 13, presided over by Cardinal Baldassarre Reina, according to Catholic News Service reporting published by the USCCB. Churches and religious communities in Rome were also invited to join in prayer. The Holy See later reported that the Pope's operation was completed successfully.
