Charbel The Movie

A feature film about Saint Charbel is real and moving toward release. Status below reflects verified public reporting as of September 20, 2026.

Announced December 2025 · In release rollout

"Charbel The Movie" (Mar Charbel)

Producer Nadim Mehanna (NMPRO Arts) announced the film on MTV Lebanon's talk show Sar El Wa2et on December 21, 2025, unveiling a trailer and a national fundraising campaign that raised more than $1.3 million to support an international release. Four actors portray Charbel across his life, with Jerry Ghazal as the younger Charbel and the story's central figure. The film is listed with Lebanese cinemas for 2026. This is a new production, distinct from the 2009 film "Charbel" by Nabil Lebbos.

Announcement coverage (Blog Baladi, December 21, 2025)

September 20, 2026 · Pilgrimage

Prayer Walk from Byblos to Annaya for South Lebanon's Christians

On September 26, pilgrims walk from Byblos to Annaya carrying the intentions of the Christians who remain in Lebanon's southern border villages - Rmeich, Ain Ebel, Debel - turning the journey into an act of solidarity and hope. Organizer Anthony Jarjour of Rmeich, who walked some 112 miles from Rmeich to Annaya last year, set out from Tyre on September 22 (about 93 miles, rerouted for road closures) to join the walk's conclusion at the shrine. The pilgrimage is organized with the Monastery of St. Maron in Annaya and the municipalities of Byblos and Annaya, with Civil Defense and Red Cross support.

Catholic World Report / EWTN News (September 20, 2026)

September 10, 2026 · Interview

The Doctor Who Investigated a St. Charbel Healing Speaks

Dr. Anne Borik, the Phoenix physician who headed the medical committee examining Dafne Gutierrez's 2016 recovery of sight after prayers at a St. Charbel relic visit, gave a new interview on how the case changed her practice of medicine. She now directs the Saint Sharbel Spiritual Life Center in Pittsburgh.

July 20, 2026 · Feast day

Hundreds Honor St. Charbel at Revesby Feast

Hundreds of faithful gathered at Revesby, Sydney, for the saint's July feast, one of many annual celebrations held by Maronite parishes around the world for the third Sunday of July (Maronite calendar) and July 24 (Latin calendar).

The Archdiocese of Cebu welcomed the Pilgrim Relic of St. Charbel Makhlouf with a Welcome Mass at San Carlos Seminary College and three days of public veneration across parishes.

Two New Miracles Reported in Early 2026

Two new miracles attributed to St. Charbel's intercession were reported in the first weeks of 2026 - one in the United States and one in Lebanon - continuing the monastery's long-running register, which now exceeds 29,680 recorded cases.

December 1, 2025 · Milestone

First Papal Visit to Saint Charbel's Tomb

Pope Leo XIV prayed at the saint's tomb in Annaya during his apostolic journey to Lebanon - the first pope ever to visit - and entrusted Lebanon, the Church, and the world to Saint Charbel's intercession.
