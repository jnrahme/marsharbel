Charbel The Movie

A feature film about Saint Charbel is real and moving toward release. Status below reflects verified public reporting as of September 20, 2026.

Announced December 2025 · In release rollout

"Charbel The Movie" (Mar Charbel)

Producer Nadim Mehanna (NMPRO Arts) announced the film on MTV Lebanon's talk show Sar El Wa2et on December 21, 2025, unveiling a trailer and a national fundraising campaign that raised more than $1.3 million to support an international release. Four actors portray Charbel across his life, with Jerry Ghazal as the younger Charbel and the story's central figure. The film is listed with Lebanese cinemas for 2026. This is a new production, distinct from the 2009 film "Charbel" by Nabil Lebbos.

September 20, 2026 · Pilgrimage

Prayer Walk from Byblos to Annaya for South Lebanon's Christians

On September 26, pilgrims walk from Byblos to Annaya carrying the intentions of the Christians who remain in Lebanon's southern border villages - Rmeich, Ain Ebel, Debel - turning the journey into an act of solidarity and hope. Organizer Anthony Jarjour of Rmeich, who walked some 112 miles from Rmeich to Annaya last year, set out from Tyre on September 22 (about 93 miles, rerouted for road closures) to join the walk's conclusion at the shrine. The pilgrimage is organized with the Monastery of St. Maron in Annaya and the municipalities of Byblos and Annaya, with Civil Defense and Red Cross support.

Catholic World Report / EWTN News (September 20, 2026)

September 27, 2026 · Beatification

Three Priests Martyred at Monte Sole to Be Beatified Sunday in Bologna

On Sunday, September 27, in the Basilica of San Petronio in Bologna, Cardinal Marcello Semeraro, prefect of the Dicastery for the Causes of Saints, will beatify three priests killed by Nazi forces in the 1944 Monte Sole massacres: Don Ubaldo Marchioni, a diocesan priest; Don Elia Comini, a Salesian; and Father Martino Capelli, a Dehonian. Because the Church has recognized them as martyrs, no miracle was required for their beatification - from Sunday they will be called Blessed. Bologna Today reports that a bullet-pierced pyx, found in the rubble of the altar of Casaglia's church, has become a symbol of the massacre the three priests did not escape.

September 24, 2026 · Beatification

Fulton Sheen Beatified in Saint Louis Before 50,000

Archbishop Fulton J. Sheen - the Emmy-winning broadcaster of Life Is Worth Living - was declared Blessed at a Mass in The Dome at America's Center in Saint Louis, Missouri. Cardinal Luis Antonio Tagle presided for Pope Leo XIV before a crowd organizers estimated near 50,000; the miracle that cleared the way was the 2010 revival of James Fulton Engstrom, who carried Sheen's relic in the procession. A day earlier, Pope Leo XIV praised Sheen's media apostolate at the Wednesday General Audience. He is Blessed, not yet a saint - his cause for canonization continues.
