Most Recent Public Miracle Reports

July 24, 2025

Naples Oil Vessel Report

A parish priest in Naples publicly described a vessel with Saint Charbel's blessed oil appearing to refill. According to the report, documentation was forwarded to Church authorities for study.

December 1, 2025

Annaya Archive Update During Papal Visit

During Pope Francis' visit to Saint Charbel's tomb in Annaya, a monastery archive coordinator stated that records contain nearly 30,000 miracle/healing claims from Christians and Muslims.
