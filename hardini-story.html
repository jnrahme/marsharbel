    About this story

    These scenes are imaginative illustrations, not historical footage. The hillside village, bookbinding hands, and classroom are composites, not a record of one afternoon. Nimatullah taught monks including Charbel, but their speech here is not invented. Children should rest, receive care, and tell a trusted adult when hurt. The final narrated page offers four points of reflection.

    Sources: Holy See biography, 1998 Holy See profile on his school and Charbel, and 2004 canonization homily. Read the fuller Nimatullah biography.
