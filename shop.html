Recommended Launch Strategy

Phase 1: Fast Launch

Use Shopify for product pages, checkout, tax, shipping, and order management. Keep fulfillment manual for the first 20-50 orders.

Phase 2: Reliable Fulfillment

Add ShipStation or Shippo for labels, tracking, and batch shipping once order volume grows.

Phase 3: Hybrid Catalog

Keep in-house items (oil, prayer cards, books) and add print-on-demand for apparel and mugs to reduce inventory risk.

Operations Standard

Define a clear SLA: process in 1-2 business days, automatic tracking email, and a simple return/refund policy page.
