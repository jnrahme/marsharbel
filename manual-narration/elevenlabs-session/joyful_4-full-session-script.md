Fourth Joyful Mystery - The Presentation.

Close your eyes and enter this mystery.
Picture the place: The Temple in Jerusalem, with prayerful reverence, candles, and sacred expectation.
Listen to the Gospel: Luke 2:22-33. Mary and Joseph present Jesus according to the Law. Simeon receives Him and praises God: "Now let your servant depart in peace." He foretells contradiction and that a sword will pierce Mary's soul. Anna also gives thanks and speaks of the child to all awaiting redemption.
In the Presentation, Jesus is offered in the Temple. Obedience, sacrifice, and prophecy meet in one holy act.
Ask for a calm heart and holy attention.
