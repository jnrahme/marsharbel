First Joyful Mystery - The Annunciation.

Scripture reading: Luke 1:26-38.
The angel Gabriel is sent to Nazareth and greets Mary: "Hail, full of grace." Mary is troubled, then listens. Gabriel reveals that she will conceive by the Holy Spirit and bear Jesus, Son of the Most High. Mary responds: "Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord. Let it be done to me according to your word."

Picture the place: Nazareth, in a modest home at dawn, with still air and soft morning light.
Watch Jesus and Mary in this moment as if you are standing nearby.
Now pray one Our Father slowly and with attention.
