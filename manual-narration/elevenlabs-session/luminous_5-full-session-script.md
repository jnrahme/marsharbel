## STEP 01 -> media/rosary/luminous_5/step-01.mp3

Fifth Luminous Mystery - Institution of the Eucharist.

Close your eyes and enter this mystery.
Picture the place: The Upper Room at Passover, table fellowship, bread and cup, and sacrificial love.
Listen to the Gospel: Matthew 26:17-20, 26-30; Luke 22:19. At the Last Supper, Jesus takes bread and wine and gives them as His Body and Blood: "Do this in remembrance of me." He establishes the sacrament that unites believers to His sacrifice and life. The Eucharist becomes the heart of Christian worship.
The Institution of the Eucharist gives the Church Christ's abiding presence: Body and Blood offered for the world.
Ask for a calm heart and holy attention.
