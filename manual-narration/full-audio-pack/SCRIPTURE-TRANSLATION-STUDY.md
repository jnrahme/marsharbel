# Scripture Translation Study For This Project

## Goal
Use scripture text that stays as close as possible to the original biblical languages while remaining Catholic and usable for Rosary narration.

## Core Language Facts
- The Old Testament was written primarily in Hebrew, with some parts in Aramaic.
- The New Testament was written in Greek.
- For Catholic reading in this project, translation should therefore be based on Hebrew/Aramaic (OT) and Greek (NT), not translated second-hand from another modern language.

## Why This Matters For "Closest To Aramaic Original"
- Aramaic is important, especially for parts of the Old Testament and ancient Near Eastern context.
- But most Old Testament text is Hebrew, and New Testament base text is Greek.
- So the best "closest to original" strategy is: faithful translation from the original-language critical texts, then narration with strong scene visualization.

## Practical Decision For This Site
- Keep the Rosary scripture source anchored to the mystery readings in `mystery-library.js` and their references.
- Use full reading text in narration stages (no shortened fallback snippets).
- Add place-and-scene cues for easier visualization (location, atmosphere, posture, prayer focus).
