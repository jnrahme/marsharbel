Reflect on this mystery with attention.

Listen to the Gospel: John 2:1-11. At a wedding feast, the wine fails. Mary brings the need to Jesus and tells the servants to obey Him. Jesus transforms water into excellent wine, and His disciples begin to believe in Him. The sign reveals both mercy and mission.

At Cana, Christ's first sign reveals His glory and Mary's maternal guidance: "Do whatever He tells you."

Ask for Trust in Jesus through Mary.
