Reflect on this mystery with attention.

Listen to the Gospel: Luke 23:13-28. Pilate yields to pressure and delivers Jesus to be crucified. Jesus carries the Cross; Simon of Cyrene is compelled to help. Women mourn as Jesus speaks to them with prophetic compassion. The road of suffering becomes the road of redemption.

On the way to Calvary, Christ carries the Cross and invites discipleship through endurance.

Ask for Patience and faithful endurance.
