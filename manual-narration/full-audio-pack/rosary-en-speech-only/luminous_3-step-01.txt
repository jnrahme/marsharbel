Reflect on this mystery with attention.

Listen to the Gospel: Mark 1:15; Mark 2:3-12; Luke 7:47-48. Jesus announces: "The kingdom of God is at hand; repent and believe in the Gospel." He forgives sins, heals the sick, and restores dignity to sinners. The kingdom is not abstract; it is Christ acting with authority, mercy, and truth in human lives.

The Proclamation of the Kingdom calls every heart to conversion, faith, and mercy.

Ask for Conversion and Christian witness.
