Reflect on this mystery with attention.

Listen to the Gospel: Luke 1:46-49; Revelation 12. Mary, humble handmaid of the Lord, is honored as Queen because of her union with Christ the King. Her Magnificat remains the song of the redeemed, and her queenship serves the salvation of her children through prayer and intercession.

The Coronation of Mary reveals the destiny of faithful love and her maternal intercession for the Church.

Ask for Perseverance and trust in Mary's intercession.
