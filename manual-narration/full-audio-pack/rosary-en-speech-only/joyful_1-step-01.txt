Reflect on this mystery with attention.

Listen to the Gospel: Luke 1:26-38. The angel Gabriel is sent to Nazareth and greets Mary: "Hail, full of grace." Mary is troubled, then listens. Gabriel reveals that she will conceive by the Holy Spirit and bear Jesus, Son of the Most High. Mary responds: "Behold, I am the handmaid of the Lord. Let it be done to me according to your word."

The Annunciation is the moment of Mary's free yes. God does not force grace; He invites. The Word takes flesh through humility and trust.

Ask for Humility and surrender to God.
