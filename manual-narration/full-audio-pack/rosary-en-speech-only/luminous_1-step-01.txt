Reflect on this mystery with attention.

Listen to the Gospel: Mark 1:1-5, 7-11. John preaches repentance and baptizes in the Jordan. Jesus comes from Nazareth and is baptized. As He rises from the water, the heavens open, the Spirit descends like a dove, and a voice declares: "You are my beloved Son; with you I am well pleased."

At the Jordan, Jesus is revealed publicly as the beloved Son, and the Trinity is made manifest.

Ask for Openness to the Holy Spirit.
