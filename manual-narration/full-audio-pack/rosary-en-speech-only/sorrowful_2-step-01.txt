Reflect on this mystery with attention.

Listen to the Gospel: John 18:28-40; 19:1. Jesus is brought before Pilate, questioned, and rejected by the crowd. Though innocent, He is handed over and scourged. The scene shows injustice, violence, and Christ's silent endurance for the salvation of souls.

The Scourging reveals Christ's innocent suffering and the cost of human sin.

Ask for Purity and healing from sin.
