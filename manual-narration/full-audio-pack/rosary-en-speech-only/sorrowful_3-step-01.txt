Reflect on this mystery with attention.

Listen to the Gospel: Mark 15:6-20. Pilate offers release, but the crowd chooses Barabbas. Soldiers clothe Jesus in purple, press thorns on His head, strike and mock Him, and lead Him to crucifixion. The true King accepts humiliation without hatred.

The Crowning with Thorns reveals mocked kingship: Christ reigns through truth, humility, and love.

Ask for Moral courage and humility.
