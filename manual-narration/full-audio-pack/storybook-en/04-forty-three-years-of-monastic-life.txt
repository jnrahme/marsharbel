Forty-Three Years of Monastic Life.

Saint Charbel spent forty-three years as a monk.
In 1875, he moved to a nearby hermitage, where he lived with fasting, work, silence, and deep prayer.

Little prayer:
God, teach me peace when life is noisy.

As you listen, picture this clearly: Visualize the hermitage at night, candlelight, silence, and steady prayer in solitude.
