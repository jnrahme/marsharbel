He Chose the Monastery.

At age twenty-three, he left home and entered the Monastery of Saint Maron at Annaya in 1851.
There he took the name Charbel and gave his life fully to God.

Little prayer:
Lord, help me choose what leads me to You.

As you listen, picture this clearly: Visualize Youssef walking toward Annaya at sunrise, leaving home with peace and courage.
