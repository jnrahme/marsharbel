A Boy in the Mountains.

Youssef Antoun Makhlouf was born on May 8, 1828, in Bekaa Kafra, Lebanon.
As a child, he loved prayer, silence, and helping people in his village.

Little prayer:
Jesus, help me find quiet moments with You.

As you listen, picture this clearly: Visualize snowy mountain air, a small village, and young Youssef quietly praying before dawn.
