Third Glorious Mystery - The Descent of the Holy Spirit.

Reflect on this mystery with attention.
Listen to the Gospel: Acts 2:1-8, 12. A mighty wind fills the house; tongues of fire rest on the apostles. They are filled with the Holy Spirit and speak so each listener hears in his own language. The Gospel begins to move publicly with power and clarity.
At Pentecost, the Spirit descends and gives birth to missionary courage and unity in the Church.
Ask for Wisdom and apostolic boldness.
