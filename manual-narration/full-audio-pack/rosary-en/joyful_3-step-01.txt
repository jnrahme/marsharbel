Third Joyful Mystery - The Nativity.

Reflect on this mystery with attention.
Listen to the Gospel: Luke 2:1-16. Joseph and Mary go to Bethlehem. There is no room at the inn, and Jesus is born and laid in a manger. Angels announce good news to shepherds: "Today a Savior has been born for you." The shepherds come in haste and adore the child with Mary and Joseph.
The Nativity shows divine glory hidden in poverty. Christ is born in simplicity and offered to the world in peace.
Ask for Detachment and holy poverty.
