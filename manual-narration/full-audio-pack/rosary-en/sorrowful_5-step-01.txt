Fifth Sorrowful Mystery - The Crucifixion.

Reflect on this mystery with attention.
Listen to the Gospel: Luke 23:33, 35-49. Jesus is crucified between two criminals. He is mocked, yet forgives and promises paradise to the repentant thief. Darkness falls; then Jesus cries out, "Father, into your hands I commit my spirit," and dies. The centurion recognizes His innocence.
At Calvary, Jesus offers His life completely and opens mercy even at the hour of death.
Ask for Self-giving love and repentance.
