Fourth Glorious Mystery - The Assumption of Mary.

Reflect on this mystery with attention.
Listen to the Gospel: 1 Corinthians 15:13-18; CCC 966. The Church professes that Mary, preserved by grace and united perfectly to her Son, is taken into heavenly glory. Her Assumption is a sign of hope for the pilgrim Church and a promise of resurrection for the faithful.
The Assumption proclaims Mary's destiny in Christ: body and soul glorified with God.
Ask for Devotion to Mary and purity of heart.
