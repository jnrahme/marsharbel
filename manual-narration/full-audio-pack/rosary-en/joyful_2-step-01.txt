Second Joyful Mystery - The Visitation.

Reflect on this mystery with attention.
Listen to the Gospel: Luke 1:39-49, 57-60. Mary travels in haste to the hill country. Elizabeth, filled with the Holy Spirit, blesses Mary and the fruit of her womb. John leaps for joy. Mary answers with the Magnificat: "My soul magnifies the Lord." The visitation becomes both proclamation and service.
The Visitation reveals love in motion. Mary carries Jesus and immediately goes to serve Elizabeth.
Ask for Charity and joyful service.
