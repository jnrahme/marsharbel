Scripture reading: Matthew 26:17-20, 26-30; Luke 22:19.
At the Last Supper, Jesus takes bread and wine and gives them as His Body and Blood: "Do this in remembrance of me." He establishes the sacrament that unites believers to His sacrifice and life. The Eucharist becomes the heart of Christian worship.
Reflect on this Word in silence.
Now pray one Our Father with attention.
