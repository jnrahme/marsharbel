Fourth Luminous Mystery - The Transfiguration.

Reflect on this mystery with attention.
Listen to the Gospel: Matthew 17:1-9. Jesus leads Peter, James, and John up a mountain. His face shines like the sun and His garments become white as light. Moses and Elijah appear with Him. A voice from the cloud proclaims: "This is my beloved Son, with whom I am well pleased; listen to Him." The disciples fall in awe and are strengthened for what is ahead.
The Transfiguration unveils Christ's divine glory and prepares disciples for the Cross.
Ask for Desire for holiness and spiritual courage.
