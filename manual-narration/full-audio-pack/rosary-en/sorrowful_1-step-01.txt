First Sorrowful Mystery - The Agony in the Garden.

Reflect on this mystery with attention.
Listen to the Gospel: Matthew 26:36-46. Jesus prays in the garden with sorrow unto death. He asks that the cup pass, yet submits fully: "Not as I will, but as You will." He finds the disciples sleeping and calls them to watch and pray. He accepts the Passion in perfect trust.
In Gethsemane, Jesus embraces the Father's will in agony, teaching obedience under suffering.
Ask for Conformity to the Father's will.
