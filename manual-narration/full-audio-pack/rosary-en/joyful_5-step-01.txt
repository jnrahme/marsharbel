Fifth Joyful Mystery - Finding Jesus in the Temple.

Reflect on this mystery with attention.
Listen to the Gospel: Luke 2:40-51. After Passover, Jesus remains in Jerusalem without His parents knowing. After three days, Mary and Joseph find Him in the Temple among teachers, listening and asking questions. Jesus says He must be in His Father's house. He then returns with them to Nazareth in obedience.
Finding Jesus in the Temple teaches persevering search. Even holy families experience anxious waiting and renewed understanding.
Ask for Perseverance in seeking Christ.
