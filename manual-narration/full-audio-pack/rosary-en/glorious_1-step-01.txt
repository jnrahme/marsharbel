First Glorious Mystery - The Resurrection.

Reflect on this mystery with attention.
Listen to the Gospel: Matthew 28:1-10, 16-20. At dawn, the women come to the tomb and find it opened. The angel announces: "He is not here; He has risen." Jesus appears to His disciples and sends them to all nations, promising: "I am with you always, to the end of the age."
The Resurrection is Christ's victory over sin and death, foundation of Christian hope.
Ask for Faith and new life.
