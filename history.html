History

Saint Charbel's Life and History

Saint Charbel Makhlouf was a Lebanese Maronite monk and priest who gave his life to silence, asceticism, and deep Eucharistic devotion. His witness still draws Christians and non-Christians from around the world.

Timeline

1828
Born Youssef Antoun Makhlouf in Bkaakafra, Lebanon (May 8, 1828).
1851
Entered the Lebanese Maronite Order and received the name Charbel.
1853
Professed monastic vows of poverty, chastity, and obedience.
1859
Ordained a priest in Bkerke after theological formation.
1875
Moved to the hermitage of Saints Peter and Paul (near Annaya), where he lived in radical solitude.
1898
Died on December 24, 1898 after suffering a stroke while celebrating Divine Liturgy - see how Saint Charbel died and the story of his tomb.
1965
Beatified by Pope Paul VI during the Second Vatican Council (December 5, 1965).
1977
Canonized by Pope Paul VI in Rome (October 9, 1977) after the Church recognized miracles attributed to his intercession. See recorded miracles.

Spiritual Signature

Silence & Solitude

Charbel embraced hiddenness and interior silence as the primary way to union with God. His hermit years became a school of listening rather than speaking.

Eucharistic Center

His priesthood was centered on the altar. Tradition strongly connects his final illness with celebration of the liturgy, underscoring the Eucharist as the core of his vocation.

Ascetic Discipline

Fasting, simplicity, and manual labor were not external show, but the framework of an undivided heart.

Universal Appeal

Devotion to Saint Charbel spread far beyond Lebanon. Pilgrims from multiple religions continue to visit Annaya, seeking healing and spiritual renewal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who was Saint Charbel?

Saint Charbel Makhlouf was a Lebanese Maronite monk and priest who gave his life to silence, asceticism, and deep Eucharistic devotion. His witness still draws Christians and non-Christians from around the world.

When and where was Saint Charbel born?

He was born Youssef Antoun Makhlouf on May 8, 1828, in Bkaakafra, Lebanon. He entered the Lebanese Maronite Order in 1851, receiving the name Charbel, and was ordained a priest in 1859.

Where did Saint Charbel live as a hermit?

From 1875 until his death he lived in radical solitude at the hermitage of Saints Peter and Paul, near Annaya, Lebanon.

When did Saint Charbel die?

He died on December 24, 1898, after suffering a stroke while celebrating the Divine Liturgy.

When was Saint Charbel canonized?

Pope Paul VI canonized him in Rome on October 9, 1977, after the Church recognized miracles attributed to his intercession. The same pope had beatified him on December 5, 1965, during the Second Vatican Council.
