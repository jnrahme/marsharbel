P3 (priest 386) - ordained priest سنة 386; twelve years preaching; verse-by-verse into daily life; "like hearing gold"; Chrysostom = فمُ الذهب، glossed "وهو باليونانية خريزوستوموس" (Greek for golden mouth, EN's own gloss). Fidelity: full.
P4 (tax riot) - riot over taxes, Lent sermons steadying the city until the emperor's pardon; lesson verbatim in sense: "الكلمات ليست للتباهي. الكلمات لخدمة الناس" = "words are not for showing off, words are for serving people". Fidelity: full.
P5 (Constantinople 398) - سنة 398, "practically against his will" = ويكاد يكون ذلك رغمًا عنه; archbishop rendered بطريركًا (OPEN-1); wealthiest court; "الله Christ is hungry in the man at the door" = المسيح جائعٌ في الإنسان الواقف عند الباب. Fidelity: full.
P6 (resistance/exile 404) - cut household spending, gave to hospitals and poor; gathering of bishops condemned him (اجتمع أساقفة وحكموا عليه - "gathering" kept generic as EN, not "council/synod" claim); people rose, returned a few days; exiled for good سنة 404; letters traveled like sermons. Fidelity: full.
P7 (the words) - direct quote kept in «»: «أتُريد أن تكرّم جسد المسيح؟ لا تهمله وهو عريان»; the "This is my body" / "I was hungry" pairing preserved; honor Christ in church and first outside in the poor; one-sentence legacy. Fidelity: full. NOTE: the quotation is EN's own rendering of the homily line; AR translates EN, not a fresh Greek sourcing (OPEN-2).
P8 (death 407) - forced marches deeper into exile; September 14 407 = الرابع عشر من أيلول سنة 407 (Levantine month per convention); Comana = كومانا; Palladius = بلاديوس remembered last words; final hymn «المجدُ لله على كلِّ شيء» (standard Arabic liturgical form of "Glory to God for all things").
