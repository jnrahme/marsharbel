Read-aloud notes: pause at the guillemets «» quotations (P7 the homily line, P8 the last words) - slow down, these are his own words. P8's closing «المجدُ للهِ على كلِّ شيء» (al-maj-du lil-LAA-hi 'a-laa kul-li shay') is the final hymn; slowest line of the book. Page titles are not read aloud. Closing intercession: يا قديس يوحنا فمَ الذهب، صلِّ لأجلنا (ya qa-DEES yoo-HAN-naa fa-ma dh-DHA-hab, SAL-lee li-AJ-li-na).
