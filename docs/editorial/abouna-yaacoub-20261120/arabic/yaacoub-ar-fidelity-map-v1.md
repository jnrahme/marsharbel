P4 (walking apostle) - 1903-1914, eleven years on foot, village after village; «رسول لبنان» = the Apostle of Lebanon (attested Arabic title for him); Syria/Palestine/Iraq/Turkey preaching kept. Fidelity: full.
P5 (one hill) - the sick/orphans/beggars/girls with no home list preserved; "المساكين" for beggars (dignified register, no mockery); "البشارة وحدها لم تكن لتكفيهم" for "preaching was not enough for them" (NOT a devaluation of preaching; matches EN intent "they needed places to be cared for"); started with one hill. P6 (Cross on the hill) - 1919, Jal el Dib = جلّ الديب north of Beirut; chapel to Our Lady of the Sea = سيّدة البحر (attested Arabic Marian title); great Cross visible to all; Franciscan Sisters of the Holy Cross of Lebanon = راهبات الصليب المقدَّس الفرنسيسيّات اللبنانيّات (attested order name - OPEN-2); works list in EN order; schools 1 -> 10 -> 230 for 7,500 students WITH EN's attribution preserved: "بحسب رهبانيّته الكبوشيّة" = "according to his Capuchin order" (institutional claim attributed, not asserted independently). Fidelity: full.
P7 (foot of the Cross) - Cross as sign of everything; sisters/works/life gathered at the Cross; "لأنّ الصليب هو حيث أحبّ يسوع الناس الذين نسيهم كل الآخرين" = EN's catechesis sentence verbatim in meaning (faith interpretation, not a historical claim). Fidelity: full.
P8 (life without reserve) - vigils/travel/illness; nearly blind; leukemia = ابيضاض الدم (standard Arabic medical term, plain not graphic); final hours as unbroken chain of prayers to the Cross and the Virgin Mary (العذراء مريم); died June 26 1954; "بكى لبنان أباه الذي كان يمشي" for "Lebanon mourned its walking father".
