## 4. Findings, page pair by page pair (EN+AR read together)

1. **Call vision (P1-2).** Throne, train filling the temple, seraphim praise, shaking doorway, smoke match Is 6:1-4 at verbatim level. The ember touching the mouth as "a sign of cleansing, preparing him" matches 6:6-7 and footnote [6:7] ("symbolically purified of sin in preparation for his mission as God's prophet"). The unclean-lips confession matches 6:5. AR: رؤيا labels match EN counts; جمرة من المذبح, علامة تطهير correct.
2. **Hardening passage (Is 6:9-10).** Never quoted. The script's summary ("They would not always listen... warnings about the harm their choices could bring") matches footnote [6:9-10]: the charge reflects the people's refusal, not a task to harden. No predestinationist reading is taught to children. Doctrinally careful.
3. **Justice message (P4).** "Learn to do good. Make justice your aim: redress the wronged, hear the orphan's plea, defend the widow" confirmed verbatim at 1:16-17. "Prayer and worship could not be separated from their treatment of a neighbor" matches 1:11-15 and footnote [1:10-17] on religious hypocrisy. AR: الأيتام والأرامل; الصلاة والعبادة لا تنفصلان عن معاملة القريب - faithful.
4. **Ahaz narrative (P5).** Syro-Ephraimite crisis, trembling "as the trees of the forest tremble in the wind" (7:2), son brought along (Shear-jashub, 7:3, unnamed in the script - acceptable simplification), the conduit of the upper pool (7:3), the call to calm trust (7:4), all confirmed. "Ahaz chose to depend on the power of Assyria" matches footnote [7:12] ("Ahaz prefers to depend upon the might of Assyria rather than the might of God"). No invented dialogue in either language.
5. **Emmanuel (P6).** The name gloss "with us is God" matches footnote [7:14] exactly. The script presents the original-context meaning first (the sign for Ahaz's fear) and the Christian hearing explicitly as Christian ("Christians would hear these words in the story of Jesus' birth"; AR «سمع المسيحيون هذه الكلمات في قصة ميلاد يسوع»). This tracks footnote [7:14]'s own Catholic hermeneutic ("to reassure Ahaz... it is understandable that this text was interpreted to refer to the birth of Christ"). "In Jesus, God truly came to live among us" is orthodox Incarnation language. The almah/parthenos question is avoided entirely, which is the right call for ages 6-12 and carries no doctrinal risk.
6. **701 BCE deliverance (P7).** The USCCB Introduction confirms Sennacherib's siege of Jerusalem in 701 BCE and that the city was spared. The script attributes the deliverance telling to the Bible ("The Bible tells how..."), so the Introduction's indemnity nuance is not contradicted. The explicit counter-prosperity line ("It does not mean that faithful people never face loss"; AR «لا تعني أنّ المؤمنين لا يواجهون الخسارة أبدًا») is present in both languages and is doctrinally important.
7. **Shoot from the stump (P8).** Is 11:1-9 confirmed: shoot from the stump of Jesse, Spirit-guided ruler judging the poor with justice, wolf guest of lamb, little child to guide them. "These are prophetic pictures, not instructions to approach wild animals" is a sound child-safety gloss, not a doctrinal hedge. Christian fulfillment labeled as Christian seeing in both languages («يرى المسيحيون»).
8. **Later disciples (P9).** The script matches the Introduction's anthology statement in sense, nearly word for word ("an anthology of poems composed chiefly by the great prophet, but also by disciples, some of whom came many years after Isaiah"). The Martyrdom/Ascension legend (sawed in two), which the Introduction itself labels "later legend," is correctly not taught. "We know little about Isaiah's final days" is honest.
9. **Reflection and prayer (P10).** Citations Is 6:8 and 1:16-17 are correct. The prayer is addressed to God ("God of hope," cf. Rom 15:13; AR «يا إلهَ الرجاء... آمين»), not to Isaiah - no intercession confusion. Petitions (courage, truth, kindness, one good deed) are orthodox and age-appropriate; the trusted-grown-up safety shape is preserved in both languages.
10. **AR faith-critical spot checks (read directly).** «الاستجابةُ لدعوةِ الله» ("responding to God's call," the resolved unambiguous rendering) present at P10. عِمَّانوئيل / «اللهُ معنا» correct. The unspoken P3 heading «هَأَنَذَا» matches the standard Arabic biblical word for Is 6:8 (Smith-Van Dyke), with provenance honestly recorded in the fidelity map and no Church-endorsement claim - acceptable; these are the name forms Arabic-speaking Christians commonly use. No martyrdom, no invented dialogue, no prosperity promise anywhere in the AR.
11. **Ruling on the flagged AR P8 modal clause (preparation item 11).** NOT a defect. EN reads "need not have the last word"; the pinned AR «وما يبدو مكسورًا ليس بالضرورة أن تكون له الكلمة الأخيرة» ("not necessarily") is the closer rendering of "need not." The interim proposal «لا ينبغي» ("should not") would also be orthodox. The pinned text that the independent v1.1 review passed stands.
12. **Same-images rule (owner rule, 2026-10-22).** The art ledger confirms one 10-plate set shared by both locales; the assembly design satisfies EN+AR same images.
