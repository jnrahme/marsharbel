# Isaiah AR narration - pronunciation and diacritics sheet v1
For the FUTURE narration voice pass (no audio authorized or generated). Fully voweled key forms, stress notes, recurrences. Narration text = the 10-section body (narration EN v1 is identical to script body text, headings unspoken [I]).

NAMES / TERMS
- إِشَعْيَاء (ʾIš-ʿa-YĀʾ) - Isaiah. Stress final. Appears throughout; keep three syllables, عْ paused.
- القُدْس (al-QUDS) - Jerusalem (site convention). Alternate biblical form أُورُشَلِيم NOT used in body; reviewer may rule.
- يَهُوذَا (Ya-HŪ-ḏā) - Judah.
- السَّرَافِيم (as-Sa-rā-FĪM) - seraphim, plural of سَرَاف; final stress.
- الْهَيْكَل (al-HAY-kal) - the Temple.
- آحَاز (ʾĀ-ḤĀZ) - Ahaz. Final stress.
- حِزْقِيَّا (Ḥiz-qiy-YĀ) - Hezekiah. Final stress, geminated يّ.
- أَشُّور (ʾAš-ŠŪR) - Assyria. Geminated شّ.
- عِمَّانُوئِيل (ʿIm-mā-nū-ʾĪL) - Emmanuel. Four syllables, final stress; gloss «اللهُ مَعَنَا» (al-LĀ-hu ma-ʿa-NĀ).
- جَمْرَة (ǦAM-ra) - ember. مَذْبَح (MAḎ-baḥ) - altar.
- نَبِيّ (na-BIYY) - prophet. رُؤْيَا (RUʾ-yā) - vision. سِفْر (SIFR) - biblical book. إِصْحَاح (ʾiṣ-ḤĀḤ) - chapter. آيَة (ʾĀ-ya) - verse.
- يَا إِلٰهَ الرَّجَاء (yā ʾi-LĀ-ha r-ra-ǦĀʾ) - prayer opener "God of hope". الرجاء = Christian hope register (matches site Arabic [I]), not الأمل.

READ-ALOUD NOTES
- P6 «اللهُ معنا»: keep the quotation marks audible as a gloss pause, not a chant.
- P10 references: "الإصحاح السادس، الآية الثامنة" then "الإصحاح الأول، الآيتين السادسة عشرة والسابعة عشرة" - read slowly; numbers are the only navigation children follow.
