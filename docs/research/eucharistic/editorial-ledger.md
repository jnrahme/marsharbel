# Worldwide Eucharistic miracle collection: editorial ledger

Compiled September 29, 2026. This is research inventory, **not 108 published articles** or a finding that every listed event was investigated or approved. The independent exhibition list is a starting corpus. Do not use the exhibition as evidence of Vatican certification.

## Scope and count

The linked Carlo Acutis / Nicola Gori exhibition lists **108 location or subject entries across 20 country/region headings** in its main country section, excluding two introductory panels and the separate 45-link "Saints, Mystics and the Eucharist" section. This count is of exhibition links, not proven supernatural events; some links describe saints or devotional events rather than host transformations, and several may refer to overlapping episodes. The 20 headings include Martinique and Réunion as separate exhibition regions, not sovereign states. Existing site articles cover Lanciano, Bolsena-Orvieto, Siena, Santarém, and Sokółka. The exhibition also lists Legnica, Tixtla, and Buenos Aires (1992/1994/1996). After deduplication and topic review, the full worldwide record may differ from 108. More recent cases may not appear in this inventory.

## Source and status rules for article drafting

- Find the actual diocese, sanctuary, episcopal communiqué, archival record or similarly direct source for each incident. Track the event date, document date, primary document, who examined it, and what they actually concluded. A curated exhibit or secondary press is a lead, not automatic Church recognition.
- State whether a bishop authorized public veneration, a diocesan inquiry took place, or a competent Church authority pronounced on supernatural character. These are different claims. A laboratory's observation does not establish divine causation. The Holy See's 2024 norms generally distinguish a `nihil obstat` and other pastoral conclusions from a declaration that an event is supernatural: https://www.vatican.va/roman_curia/congregations/cfaith/documents/rc_ddf_doc_20240517_norme-fenomeni-soprannaturali_en.html . Do not map historical events mechanically onto those new categories.
- Keep allegations, devotional traditions, and uncertain dates clearly attributed; do not promise that all worldwide events are known or that all exhibition items have the same evidentiary standing.
- Research photo rights per asset. The exhibition and sanctuary photos are not freely reusable simply because they are online. A license ledger must name image, creator, source URL, exact license, permission/attribution, and whether the object/setting truly depicts the case. Do not publish a page with an unlicensed image.
- English is the source copy; seven locale pages should mirror story content and sources, with complete translation and hreflang only once they actually exist. Registry, sitemap, locale routing, SEO mapping, tests and two real rendered viewport reviews are part of each shipped batch.

## First case for a sourced story: Legnica, Poland

- The Sanctuary of St. Hyacinth publishes Bishop Zbigniew Kiernikowski's communiqué: https://www.jacek-legnica-sanktuarium.pl/en/miracles/curia-message . It dates the fallen consecrated host to December 25, 2013, notes red discoloration after placement in water, describes a predecessor's observation commission, cites forensic findings of tissue "most similar to cardiac muscle" and human-origin genetic findings, and says he consulted the Congregation for the Doctrine of the Faith in January before directing display in accord with Holy See recommendations. The communiqué calls the event one with the *hallmarks* of a Eucharistic miracle; do not rewrite it as a Vatican declaration of supernatural origin.
- The Diocese of Legnica's 2026 anniversary notice reports public veneration on July 2, 2016 with Holy See agreement: https://diecezja.legnica.pl/zaproszenia/10-lat-obecnosci,1056 . Its July 2026 retrospective: https://diecezja.legnica.pl/aktualnosci/relacje,1/10-lat-od-wydarzenia-o-znamionach-cudu,3825 . These substantiate continuing public devotion, not independent laboratory replication.
- Exhibition row: https://www.miracolieucaristici.org/en/liste/scheda_b.html?nat=polonia&wh=legnica . That page is an exhibit access point, not the original authority.
