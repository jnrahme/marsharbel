# Store policy drafts (for Quality, Trust & Risk review; not published)

These are drafts. Joey is the seller of record and approves final wording. Not legal advice.

## Shipping
Every item is made to order and shipped by a partner print facility, usually from the region nearest to the buyer. Production takes a few business days, then delivery time depends on the destination. A tracking link is emailed when the order ships. Shipping cost is shown at checkout before payment.

## Returns and refunds
Items are printed only after you order, so we cannot accept returns or exchanges for change of mind or wrong size. If an item arrives damaged, defective or misprinted, report it within 30 days of delivery with a photo and we will arrange a free reprint or a refund. If a parcel is returned or lost because the address was wrong, only the production cost is refunded. (Mirrors Printify's standard policy for Pop-Up stores: help.printify.com article 12051129478417. Confirm before publishing.)

## Privacy
We share only what is needed to make and ship your order (name, address, email for tracking) with our print and payment partners. We do not sell your data. Link to the site privacy policy.

## Terms and claims
Souvenirs are keepsakes. They are not relics and do not promise healing, favors or any spiritual outcome. Artwork is original to marsharbel.com; saint images from third parties are not used without a recorded licence.
