# 2026-09-25 - Polish prayer mirror

`/pl/modlitwy` replaces the short Polish reading guide with a complete 114-slot Polish catalog through the English prayer master. The generated page retains seven visible sections, the historic photo, 14 cards and seven complete Rosary texts, and keeps canonical `/pl/modlitwy`, the existing reciprocal hreflang cluster, sitemap and selector. Edit `locales/pl/mirrors/prayers.json`, not the generated HTML. The Polish page was the final shorter guide in this prayer-page cluster; this does not imply the other topic pages are mirrors.

The intercessions, nine-day novena framework and short invocations are original site compositions translated into Polish, not attributed to the monastery. For traditional prayer forms, see the [Polish bishops' unified prayer-text collection](https://opoka.org.pl/files/26.bkt/03/04/16/637806_cwyE_iconemodlitwytekstintegralny.pdf), [Opoka's Apostles' Creed, Our Father, Hail Mary and Glory Be](https://opoka.org.pl/biblioteka/M/MP/modlitwy_p1), [WAM's Rosary sequence](https://opoka.org.pl/biblioteka/T/TS/wam_2013_rozaniec_01), [the Fatima Secretariat's published approved invocation](https://sekretariatfatimski.pl/index.php/aktualnosci/1073-biskupi-ujednolicili-teksty-modlitw-w-tym-rowniez-modlitw-fatimskich), and [the Pallottines' closing collect](https://ordo.pallotyni.pl/index.php/naboestwa-i-modlitwy/modlitwy-maryjne/1037-rozaniec-rosarium). The Apostles' Creed is not substituted by the Nicene Creed. No guaranteed healing or miracle is claimed.
