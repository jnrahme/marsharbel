# 2026-09-25 - Arabic prayer mirror pilot

## Scope and rollout state

- The canonical English `/saint-charbel-prayers` and Arabic `/ar/prayers` use one template (`templates/mirrors/prayers.html`) with 114 semantic text slots per locale in `locales/{en,ar}/mirrors/prayers.json`. The English catalog is the master for the page shape. Layout, section order, photo, prayer cards, CSS and behavior are shared. The builder fails on missing, extra, empty or HTML-bearing slots. No runtime translation fills a published mirror.
- The published Arabic short prayer guide is replaced only for this page. The remaining `/en/prayers`, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, German and Polish prayer guides are still shorter guides. Hreflang remains the existing reciprocal language cluster. Hreflang is not a claim of content parity. The English master retains its own existing self/x-default hreflang pair; the static English reading guide remains at `/en/prayers` until its separate rollout. No slugs or sitemap entries change. Language switching on the Arabic mirror returns to the full English master, not to the short English guide.
- `translate.js` uses the existing `#sc-language-select`. The Arabic mirror ignores stale English localStorage/query overrides, so the document remains authored Arabic. Existing runtime-only languages retain fallback behavior and are not emitted as standalone SEO routes. The older directory-hub selector fix from separate commit `6e7cccd` is incorporated in this branch's generalized route lookup and its two tests.
- Generator output is editable through catalogs/template, never by hand. `scripts/preview-prayer-mirror.py --out /tmp/prayer-preview` creates a local review copy outside the repo.

## Sacred text and editorial notes

The 4 original Saint Charbel intercession prayers, 3 novena items, 4 short invocations and their framing have been translated as this site's original devotional compositions, not attributed to the monastery. The Rosary section keeps **Apostles' Creed**, the English master's actual prayer, rather than substituting the Nicene Creed from the Maronite Cairo rosary page. Seven prayer titles and complete prayer texts are present. The Arabic closing prayer is an original Arabic rendering of the English prayer; it should not be represented as a copied authorized Arabic edition. Usage and prayer-form checks: [Maronite Eparchy of Cairo Rosary guide](https://www.maronite-egypt.com/prayers-rosary) (Our Father, Hail Mary, Glory Be, Fatima), [Apostles' Creed](https://rosary.ipray.eu/credo_apostolico_ar.htm), [Saint Rafqa Hail Holy Queen](https://www.saintrefqa.com/%D8%B5%D9%84%D8%A7%D8%A9-%D8%A7%D9%84%D8%B3%D9%84%D8%A7%D9%85-%D8%B9%D9%84%D9%8A%D9%83-%D8%A3%D9%8A%D8%AA%D9%87%D8%A7-%D8%A7%D9%84%D9%85%D9%84%D9%83%D8%A9/), [Archdiocese of Washington English Rosary closing form](https://adw.org/catholic-prayer/rosary-prayer/). Local wording variants are expected. Neither source text nor page claims prayer guarantees a cure.
