# 2026-09-25 - French prayer mirror

The French `/fr/prieres` guide is replaced with the complete English-master prayer page through the shared keyed template. It has the same historic photo, seven direct sections, 14 prayer cards and seven complete Rosary prayers; French text lives in `locales/fr/mirrors/prayers.json` (114 keys). The existing canonical slug, sitemap listing, reciprocal hreflang cluster and single top language selector are unchanged. Other languages' prayer guides remain short until their mirrors pass independently. The generator owns this page; don't edit `fr/prieres.html` directly.

Editorial: the four intercession prayers, three novena elements and four short invocations are translated original compositions of this site, not attributed to the monastery. The traditional Rosary forms were checked against the [French Catholic bishops' Apostles' Creed](https://eglise.catholique.fr/approfondir-sa-foi/prier/prieres/372210-credo-symbole-des-apotres/), [Our Father](https://eglise.catholique.fr/approfondir-sa-foi/prier/prieres/372214-notre-pere/), [Hail Mary](https://eglise.catholique.fr/approfondir-sa-foi/prier/prieres/372212-je-vous-salue-marie/) and [Salve Regina](https://eglise.catholique.fr/approfondir-sa-foi/prier/prieres/383276-salve-regina-salut-o-reine/) pages, the [Vatican French Rosary text](https://www.vatican.va/special/rosary/documents/misteri_fr.html) and the [Ontario Catholic faith office's closing prayer](https://www.opeco.ca/prieres/rosaire). Keep the Apostles' Creed, not the Nicene Creed. Local wording variants exist; no cure or miracle is promised. Text is rendered as reviewed devotional content, not claimed to be an official edition.
