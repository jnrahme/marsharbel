# Faverney research and photo, September 29, 2026

The Catholic Church in France's 2008 anniversary account explicitly reports an archbishop of Besançon's judgment of authenticity on July 10, 1608 following an episcopal inquiry and 54 written depositions: https://eglise.catholique.fr/actualites/365023-400eme-anniversaire-du-miracle-eucharistique-de-faverney/ . The municipal timeline of the fire and reported suspended ostensory is https://www.ville-faverney.com/histoire-patrimoine/le-miracle-de-1608/59-1608-les-saintes-hosties-sauvees-des-flammes . This editor has not inspected the original depositions/decree, so the page attributes this finding to the bishops' conference rather than citing it as firsthand archival research. The testimony was of an event in 1608, not a modern laboratory finding.
