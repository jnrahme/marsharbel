# 2026-09-26 - Qadisha English geography correction

The live English `/qadisha-valley` master conflated two places: Charbel's birthplace, Bekaa Kafra near Bsharri and the Qadisha Valley, and his later Hermitage of Saints Peter and Paul at Annaya in the Jbeil district. It described the hermitage as above Bsharri overlooking the valley and Annaya's monastery as on the ridge above the valley. Corrected the Saint Charbel section and its FAQ answer to put the Annaya hermitage above the Saint Maron monastery there, apart from Qadisha. The FAQPage structured answer was changed to match the visible correction. No other Qadisha passages or URL were changed.

Checked [Annaya monastery: the Hermitage of Saints Peter and Paul is the Annaya hermitage](https://saintcharbel.com/the-hermitage/), [the monastery's Annaya/Jbeil location](https://saintcharbel.com/monastery/), and [UNESCO's Qadisha location in north Lebanon, west of the Cedars near Bcharré](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/850/). A separate [papal-visit background page](http://www.popeinlebanon.com/en/news/Monastery-of-Saint-Maron-Annaya-Tomb-of-Saint-Charbel) explicitly places the Saints Peter and Paul hermitage on the hill opposite the Annaya monastery. These are distinct sites; no claimed view from the hermitage across Qadisha remains.

The two corrected English paragraphs are split into plain-text semantic slots in `locales/en/qadisha-corrections.json`. `scripts/i18n/qadisha_copy.py` checks their exact HTML and FAQ structured answer; the policy exempts only those validated slots, rather than expanding the frozen legacy baseline. This is a focused bridge until the Qadisha master is converted to the shared mirror template. The latter rollout is held until this English correction is live.

Checks: `npm run i18n:check`; `npm run i18n:test`; `python3 scripts/qa/check_seo.py`; `python3 scripts/sitemap_lastmod.py --check`. Local desktop (1366px) and phone (390px) screenshots were inspected at the corrected section and FAQ; text wraps cleanly without horizontal overflow. No deployment by this branch.
