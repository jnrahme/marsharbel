# marsharbel.com SEO angle map v1 (Oct 2 2026)
Basis: static audit of all 267 sitemap URLs on stage 1c25b98 (page-level CSV attached to the PR), the Sept 30 production crawl (430 refs, all 200), GSC UI (35 clicks / 1.3k impressions, 7d to Sept 29), GA by hostName (real traffic ~10/day avg, ~30/day recent), Trends demand map, AEO lane list (35 items). Core Web Vitals are NOT measured here (HTTP crawl only); field data needs CrUX/PSI.
Scoring: Impact H/M/L x Effort S/M/L. Wave 1 = do now through the merge train. "Needs Joey" = account, outreach or public posting.

## A. Page-by-page audit facts (267 URLs: 129 EN, ar 28, es 21, fr 20, pt 18, de/it/pl 17 each)
- Titles: 56 pages over 60 chars (all 20 rosary mysteries pages, chaplet, 22nd page); descriptions: 41 over 160. Every page has 1 h1, canonical, description, OG image, schema.
- Thin EN pages under 350 words: hardini-story 175, charbel-story-v2 186, six eucharistic-miracle pages 270-294 (siena, bolsena-orvieto, sokolka, santarem, lanciano, legnica area), become-like-charbel 278, joseph-story 328, anthony-story 338. Locale avg words: EN 861 vs pl 358, de 402, it 414, pt 433 (locale pages are 40-50% of EN depth).
- Internal links (counted from crawl refs): median 3 inbound links per page; 134 pages have 3 or fewer; accessibility and terms 0. Weak: all 20 rosary mystery pages (1 each), 10 eucharistic pages (1), story pages (1), st-* saint pages (1), nohad/dafne/raymond (2).
- Schema: FAQPage 87 pages, Article 85, TouristAttraction 20, Person 12, VideoObject 1. 87 pages carry BreadcrumbList/Organization; zero sameAs anywhere. 16 pages with video embeds have no VideoObject (nohad-el-shami, dafne-gutierrez, canonization, raymond-nader, latest-register-entries, testimonies, voice-testimony, st-padre-pio...).
- Images: 362 imgs; 16 pages have an image with no alt (story pages, gallery); no image sitemap; 54 PNGs over 300KB and 6 JPGs over 400KB in repo (check what is actually served); gallery not tied to image-search metadata.
- Sitemap 266 URLs, no image or video extensions, no lastmod audit done yet. hreflang present with x-default on checked page (ar, de, en, es, fr, it, pl, pt). robots.txt welcomes GPTBot, OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, ClaudeBot, Googlebot. llms.txt exists (slugs need a check: it lists /en/biography, /en/prayers, /en/novena that may redirect).
- Demand reality: Lebanon demand is the name ("charbel" 49, "شربل" 39, "مار شربل" 22-30), not modifiers; PL nowenna and BR novena show far more total prayer demand; kids stories read 0.
