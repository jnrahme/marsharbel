# Community sharing pack — drafts, not sent

## Purpose
Offer useful Saint Charbel resources to relevant communities after the pages are published and verified. Do not buy links, mass-message directories, imply Church endorsement, or ask for links unrelated to the recipient's readers. All external messages require Joey's explicit instruction identifying the recipients.

## Candidate audiences to research

- Maronite parish and eparchy resource editors serving readers of the eight supported languages.
- Catholic prayer groups and newsletters with an existing Saint Charbel or Rosary audience.
- Faith-formation and family ministry teams seeking accessible reading resources.

Confirm each recipient's current public contact route, editorial policy, and audience before outreach. No personal contact list has been scraped or created.
