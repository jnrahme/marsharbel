Pray the Sorrowful Mysteries with us on Tuesday, October 13, 2026. Each Hail Mary is paired with a short visualization to help you contemplate the mystery. We begin by remembering Saint Edward the Confessor and asking his prayers.

Saint Edward is remembered on October 13 in the England calendar; this is a weekday in the US general calendar, not a universal obligatory memorial.
