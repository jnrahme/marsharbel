# Mother Teresa child story: source and representation notes

The story is an original retelling, not a copied transcript or recorded speech. Its illustrated, narrated ten-page build is prepared on a production branch and is not yet merged or published. It combines documented history with careful invitations to imagine ordinary scenes. The "room where a child is learning" is explicitly an imaginative composite, not a witnessed event. The "call within a call" is Mother Teresa's reported interior experience, not a voice other train passengers heard. Statements about her spiritual darkness paraphrase the Vatican biography's discussion of her letters; do not equate this with every child's emotions or imply that seeking help is wrong.

- Holy See, biography for her 2003 beatification: https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/saints/ns_lit_doc_20031019_madre-teresa_en.html . Supports 1910 Skopje birth, parents, early bereavement, 1928 Loreto entry, school teaching and principal role, 1946 reported call, 1948 permission/sari/medical training/neighborhood care, 1950 Missionaries of Charity, the 1979 Nobel, spiritual darkness, 1997 death.

This is the community's account, not an independent historical audit.
- Nobel Prize, 1979 Peace Prize biographical record: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/peace/1979/teresa/biographical/ . Award and context, not proof every aspect of her work solved suffering.

## Reflection outro (Joey's September 27 direction)
A separate narrated tenth page presents four short, concrete reflection points. Each pairs a documented choice in Teresa's life (noticing people, learning practical care, working with others, persisting through reported spiritual darkness) with a Christian teaching and a child-sized action/question. The neighbor-love framing is from Jesus' teaching in Luke 10:25-37 and Matthew 25:35-40; it is an application, not a claim that an exact exchange occurred. The darkness point avoids telling children to conceal distress and explicitly allows honest conversation and asking for help. It reuses the child-takeaway illustration.
