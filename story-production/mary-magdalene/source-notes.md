# Mary Magdalene research and editorial notes

Original retelling, nine narrative pages in paragraph order plus four-point narrated reflection. No invented character dialogue. The lakeside road, dawn atmosphere, and Mary's imagined walk are explicitly imaginative composites. The direct quotation "I have seen the Lord" is John 20:18. No promised miracle. The crucifixion is non-graphic, and the script names rest and trusted adults.

- Luke 8:1-3 for Mary of Magdala as disciple, healing and support of Jesus' ministry; John 19:25 and 20:1-18 for cross, tomb, tears, encounter and witness. USCCB New American Bible: https://bible.usccb.org/bible/luke/8 , https://bible.usccb.org/bible/john/19 , https://bible.usccb.org/bible/john/20 . These pages were fetched to confirm the chapter references.
- Holy See press office, decree explanation (2016): https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2016/06/10/160610c.html . Fetched. Explains apostle-of-apostles title, July 22 feast, first witness and historical conflation with the unnamed anointing woman and Mary of Bethany. The script does not conflate them or call her a prostitute.
- Pope Francis, General Audience (May 17, 2017): https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/audiences/2017/documents/papa-francesco_20170517_udienza-generale.html . Fetched. Explains Mary first assumed a theft, returned to the tomb, grieved, recognized Jesus when called by name, then witnessed to the disciples.

Historical cautions: The Gospels differ in how they narrate women at the tomb; this story follows John's account for sequence and uses Luke for earlier discipleship. The first-witness wording is specific to John's named encounter and the Holy See's teaching, not a claim that no other woman was present in every Gospel account. Exact appearance of Mary and the garden is unknown. Reflection is pastoral application, not a quotation from Mary.

Setting opening: In the first century, in Galilee beside its great lake, Mary Magdalene lived in the time of Jesus. The era follows the sources above; this does not assign an exact year to an undocumented scene.
