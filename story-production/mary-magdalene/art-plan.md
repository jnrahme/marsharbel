# mary-magdalene: illustration plan (script approved)

Ten unique 16:9 painterly watercolor/gouache story illustrations, warm cream/bronze/blue, joyful and gentle but not sentimental. These are imaginative historical composites rather than documentary images. Consistent protagonist in age, hairstyle, clothing and ethnicity within the volume; no illustration reused from another saint's book. No readable lettering or speech bubbles. Narration and reflection remain HTML text, never baked into art. Dignified historically plausible clothing and architecture; no copying photographs or artworks. Avoid gore, weapons in foreground, miracle beams, invented dialogue and implied cure. Page 10 clearly modern reflection scene. Inspect every image's pixels for anatomy, continuity, historical claims, and child safety before acceptance.
