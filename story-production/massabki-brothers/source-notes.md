# Massabki Brothers - sources and safety
Draft checkpoint 2026-09-28. Nine pages, four-point reflection. Original prose; market and door illustrations are historical composites. No invented dialogue or re-enactment of violence.

- Holy See Dicastery 23 May 2024 decree: https://press.vatican.va/content/salastampa/en/bollettino/pubblico/2024/05/23/240523b.html . Names Francis, Abdel Mohti and Raphaël and recognizes martyrdom 9-10 July 1860 in Damascus with eight Franciscan friars.
- Holy See canonization Mass 20 October 2024: https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/homilies/2024/documents/20241020-omelia-canonizzazione.html . Lists Francesco, Mooti and Raffaele alongside Manuel Ruiz and seven companions. Naming transliteration varies: script uses Francis/Mooti/Raphael and notes Mooti is Abdel Mohti/Abdel Moati.
- Order of Friars Minor account: https://ofm.org/en/the-holy-martyrs-of-damascus.html . Distinct biographies: Francis a silk merchant, married with eight children, generosity; Mooti married with five, teaches at friary school and prays there; Raphael unmarried, assists family/friars, devotion to Mary. Attacked friary and martyrdom, joint burial. This source is church primary institutional history, although its historical attribution of the 1860 attacks is compressed and potentially confuses Lebanese and Damascus events.
- Maronite Eparchy of Australia: https://maronite.org.au/news/massabki-brothers-canonisation-date-announced/ . Confirms names, lay vocation, martyrdom, 2024 canonization. Its description of an "Ottoman attack" differs from Franciscan and other accounts; script avoids naming perpetrators as an ethnic or religious group, and does not make an unsupported military claim.

Child safety: no swords, wounds, attackers' faces, or dramatic martyr dialogue. Explicitly state violence was specific people's actions, not an entire faith group. Do not teach children to remain in danger; seeking safety and adults is named. The phrase "told to give up their Christian faith" is supported by Franciscan narrative; exact words are not presented as historical quotes. Canonical date is October 20, 2024.
