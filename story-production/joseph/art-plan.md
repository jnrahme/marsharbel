# Joseph: original illustrated storybook art plan (script approved by parent)

Ten original painterly 16:9 illustrations, warm cream/amber/olive/sky blue, luminous but no supernatural light beams. This is an imaginative visual retelling, not archaeological reconstruction or documentary evidence. A consistent Joseph: adult Middle Eastern Jewish man, dark hair/beard, earth-colored simple tunic and cloak; Mary: adult Middle Eastern Jewish woman in muted blue/brown wrap; Jesus: infant through child age twelve. Never use existing saints' artwork. Historical setting draws on the Gospel's named places only. No speech bubbles, text, modern artifacts, weaponry, Herod confrontation, disaster, or glamourized unsafe travel. Text stays in HTML, not image.

1. Nazareth craft: adult Joseph beside simple woodworking bench, sunlight on stone streets. Composite workshop, no named tools as a historical claim.
2. Compassionate choice: Joseph and adult Mary standing at separate sides of gentle courtyard; interior dream motif kept symbolic, no pictured angel as witnessed portrait.
3. Bethlehem birth: Mary, Joseph, swaddled baby by simple manger, humble shelter; no assertion of exact room design.
4. Naming Jesus: Joseph tenderly holding baby beside Mary; no speech depicted.
5. Flight: Joseph, Mary and bundled baby traveling on foot along broad road toward distant hills; everyone clothed, calm, traveling together. No soldiers or danger shown.
6. Returning to Nazareth: family walking toward simple hill town, Jesus older toddler; invented composite landscape.
7. Work at home: Joseph and young Jesus near carpentry bench with Mary nearby; avoid power tools or hazardous activity.
8. Temple search: Mary and Joseph find twelve-year-old Jesus seated among adult teachers in ancient colonnade; avoid losing child visual distress.
9. Quiet life: Joseph, Mary, and adolescent Jesus walk through Nazareth at dusk; no invented death tableau.
10. Reflection: modern child and trusted adult doing simple repair together, one open blank area for reflection HTML overlay; clear era contrast.
