# Joseph: script checkpoint and sources

Original narrated retelling, nine narrative pages and four-point reflection outro. No art or audio approved yet. Biblical events are attributed to Matthew or Luke; the workshop and travel impressions are explicitly imagined composites, not eyewitness claims. No invented dialogue is assigned to Joseph or Mary. Direct second-person quotation in page four is an authorial explanation of naming, not asserted historical speech. The script avoids claims of Joseph's death, age, appearance, birthplace, or exact trade tools, which the cited passages do not establish.

- Pope Francis, *Patris Corde* (8 Dec 2020), https://www.vatican.va/content/francesco/en/apost_letters/documents/papa-francesco-lettera-ap_20201208_patris-corde.html . Fetched 28 Sep 2026. Intro and sections 3, 5, 6: Joseph the carpenter, a just man; Mary, dreams, Bethlehem, Egypt and Nazareth, finding the twelve-year-old Jesus; his discreet, creative courage and work. The letter explicitly notes how little the Gospels say about him.
- Gospel of Matthew 1:18-25 and 2:13-23, as referenced and recounted in *Patris Corde*: Mary's pregnancy, dream, name Jesus, departure for Egypt, return to Nazareth. Distinguish Gospel report from visual inference.
- Gospel of Luke 2:1-7 and 2:41-52, as referenced and recounted in *Patris Corde*: Bethlehem, manger, Temple search and Nazareth return. Luke records Mary's concern; no other dialogue invented.

Child safety: flight is presented as seeking safety rather than an invitation to travel alone; lost-child page directs a child to trusted adults; responsibilities belong to caregivers, not children. No promises of divine warning or rescue for readers.
