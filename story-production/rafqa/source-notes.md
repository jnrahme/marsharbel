# Rafqa storybook research and editorial notes

This is an original child-suitable retelling, not a copied transcript or a claim of eyewitness dialogue. It is a script checkpoint for review; no art or narration is yet published. The kitchen and knitting details are explicitly imaginative composites based on documented kinds of work. No character speech, miracle promise, or medical diagnosis is invented. Do not frame pain as desirable or tell children to refuse treatment. The violence scene is attributed to the Holy See account and kept non-graphic. Boutroussieh's experience before the Marian icon and her later dream are reported inner experiences, not public events.

- Holy See, 2001 biography: https://www.vatican.va/news_services/liturgy/documents/ns_lit_doc_20010610_rafqa-choboq_en.html . Supports childhood, mother loss, household work, Bikfaya, Ghazir kitchen and study, Deir el-Kamar child protection, Maad school, 1871 move/name, illness and medical treatment, 1897 Jrabta move, handicraft, 1914 death. The page has a clear chronology typo: four years after 1843 says she returned in 1841. This script omits the disputed return date.
- St Rafqa monastery biography: https://www.rafqa.com/Biography-Saint-Rafqa . First-party devotional source corroborating work, schooling and illness; quotes and miracles are not retold as independently verified facts.
- St Rafqa monastery history: https://www.rafqa.com/Convent-Saint-Rafqa . Details of six-nun transfer in 1897 and shared work; not a reason to imply Rafqa herself planted while incapacitated.
- Pope John Paul II, canonization homily (June 10, 2001): https://www.vatican.va/content/john-paul-ii/en/homilies/2001/documents/hf_jp-ii_hom_20010610_canonizzazione.html . Confirms canonization, highlights love for her people, reconciliation, support for sufferers.

Reflection is an authored pastoral application of Jesus' teachings about care for vulnerable neighbors (e.g. Matthew 25:35-40), not a quotation from Rafqa or a claim that every detail was witnessed. Child safety: explicitly seek care and adult help.
