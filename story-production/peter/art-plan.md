# Saint Peter illustrated storybook: scene plan (script green-lit 2026-09-28)

All images are imaginative painterly 16:9 scenes, never documentary. Warm Galilean palette: lake blues, dawn apricot, cream stone, fishing-boat wood browns. Mood warm and hopeful. Continuity: Peter is a sturdy middle-aged Galilean fisherman with weathered skin, dark graying hair and beard, simple earth-toned tunic; Jesus is calm, in a simple off-white robe; first-century fishing boats with square sails; Capernaum/Bethsaida stone houses. No gore, no crucifixion depiction, no rooster close-ups, no chains biting skin; the denial page shows emotion without spectacle; the angel on page 8 is a softly painted luminous figure, clearly painterly, not photographic; no beams of divine light.
