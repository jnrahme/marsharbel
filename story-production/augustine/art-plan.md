# Saint Augustine of Hippo art plan (checked against locked script v4, 2026-10-04)

Final art converted to 1280x720 WebP into media/storybook-augustine/images/. Painterly realistic illustration, same house style as Marina, Maroun and Sergius and Bacchus. Budget: 12 slots (10 pages + 2 spare), allocated 2026-10-04.

**Palette:** North African and Italian late Roman towns: warm limestone and plaster, olive and fig green, terracotta, deep Mediterranean blue, soft golden or lamp light.

**Era:** 4th century Roman North Africa and Milan (dates are documented: 354 to 430). Plain late-Roman clothing: undyed or dyed wool tunics, women in long tunics with a head veil, simple sandals. No armour, no crowns, no modern items on pages 1-9. A bishop wears a plain long robe, no mitre.

**Characters (same faces across pages):** AUGUSTINE: olive skin, dark curly short hair; boy about 10 (p1), teenager (p2 only in window), young man about 19 (p3), 30-32 (p4-7, clean-shaven or a light stubble), about 60 and weary with short grey hair and light grey beard (p8-9). MONICA: woman with olive skin, dark hair under a plain cream veil, kind lined face, about 35 on p1 and about 55 later. AMBROSE: older bishop, short dark hair, clean-shaven, plain long robe. ALYPIUS: young man, Augustine's friend, only far in the background.

**Hard negatives (every prompt):** no text, letters, captions or inscriptions; no halos or glowing light effects; no modern objects in pages 1-9; no armour, weapons or violence; no nudity (baptism: white tunic, waist-deep water, back view); no blood; no crowns or mitres; no Gothic or Western medieval architecture.

1. Morning in Tagaste, a small hill town in Roman North Africa. MONICA kneels beside AUGUSTINE, a bright curious boy about 10, at a flat rooftop wall; olive trees and plain stone houses below, far blue sea. Two figures, affectionate, warm light.
2. Night inside a plain stone room. MONICA kneels in prayer, hands folded, one small oil lamp, calm face. Through the open window a pear tree and the moon, and the faint silhouettes of three boys beneath it. No one else in the room.
3. A sunny Roman lecture hall in the city of Carthage with a harbour glimpsed through arches. AUGUSTINE, about 19, stands speaking to a few seated young students in tunics, one hand raised. Busy, bright, confident; his face a little searching.
4. The interior of a plain Roman basilica in Milan, columns and a high wooden roof, light from tall windows. Bishop AMBROSE preaches from a low raised platform; AUGUSTINE, about 31, stands among listeners with his head tilted, attentive. Not a Gothic church.
5. A walled garden in Milan at late afternoon. AUGUSTINE, about 31, has thrown himself down under a fig tree and covers his face with his hands in tears; a long way back, ALYPIUS sits quietly on a stone bench, not looking. A fig tree, a small stone house wall. Sorrowful, tender, no light effects.
6. The same fig-tree garden, same hour. AUGUSTINE stands up holding an open old book (a late-antique codex, pages with only faint unreadable marks), looking at it with wide quiet eyes; a window in the neighbouring house is just visible, no child shown. Light softens, calm.
7. A stone baptistery in Milan at Easter dawn. AMBROSE, in a plain white robe, stands waist-deep in a stone pool and pours water over the head of AUGUSTINE (back view, white tunic). MONICA, in a cream veil, watches from the edge with folded hands and a calm face; a few witnesses in white. Soft morning light.
8. A harbour town church doorway in Hippo, afternoon. AUGUSTINE as a bishop (about 60, grey, plain long robe) hands bread from a basket to a few poor people, an old woman and a thin boy; two monks in simple tunics help beside him. Warm, kind, ordinary.
9. Evening in a simple room in Hippo. Old AUGUSTINE, thin, sits propped in a plain chair by a window and gazes at several loose sheets pinned to the wall beside him (only blurred marks, no readable writing); two monks sit quietly nearby. Through the window, distant city walls at dusk. Peaceful and dignified, no soldiers, no violence.
10. A present-day scene (modern, flagged as such in the page copy): a girl of about 9 with dark curly hair and her grandparent sit side by side on a bench on a seaside hill at sunset, quiet, looking at the sea; modern clothing. Peaceful, warm, hopeful.

Every image is an imaginative composite, not documentary or an eyewitness scene. Pixel-checked against page text before acceptance. Rejections are regenerated within the 12-slot budget; nothing beyond 12.
