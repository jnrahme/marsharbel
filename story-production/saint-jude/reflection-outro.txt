What would you want to ask Jesus if you were at that table? You do not need to hurry toward an answer.

Read Jude 20-22 with a parent or another trusted adult. The passage connects prayer and God's love with mercy toward people who doubt.

Today, listen to someone's question without laughing at it. You can also bring one of your own questions to someone who cares for you.

God, help us come to you honestly and treat one another with mercy. Saint Jude, pray for us as we learn to follow Jesus. Amen.
