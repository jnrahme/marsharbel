# Caregiver notes

Illustrations are original imaginative composites, not documentary reconstructions. No private conversation is invented.

Illness and grief: children should tell a trusted adult when hurting or ill, rest and receive medical care. Prayer does not replace treatment or guarantee a cure. Grief need not be hurried. Children are not asked to imitate illness or harmful sacrifice.

Simple reflection actions, such as helping with a chore or listening at home, should fit the child's age and household.
