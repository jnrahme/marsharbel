# Saints Sergius and Bacchus art plan

Style and negatives: see prompts below. Final art converted to 1280x720 WebP into media/storybook-sergius-bacchus/images/.

**Palette:** Roman Empire around the year 300: warm marble cream, terracotta red, bronze, Syrian desert gold, deep sky blue.

**Characters:** SERGIUS: a Roman officer of about 35 with short dark brown hair, clean-shaven, strong calm face, wearing a bronze-colored cuirass over a crimson tunic and a red cloak. BACCHUS: a Roman officer of about 30 with short black curly hair and a short dark beard, a slightly gentler face, same armor and crimson tunic with a blue cloak. Same faces and clothes in every scene; stay consistent; after page 3 they wear plain undyed tunics instead of armor.

1. Interior of a Roman imperial palace colonnade with cream marble columns and mosaic floor, warm light. SERGIUS and BACCHUS stand side by side as officers of the guard, relaxed and friendly, shoulders close, smiling at each other. In the far background, small figures of courtiers and an emperor in a purple robe walking away. Spears stand in a rack, not held.
2. Exterior of a grand Roman temple with tall columns and wide steps, late afternoon. In the distance, a purple-robed emperor and a crowd of soldiers walk up the steps into the temple. Off to the side, outside among the columns in shadow, SERGIUS and BACCHUS stand with heads bowed and hands folded in quiet prayer. Their calm contrasts with the busy crowd.
3. A Roman throne room with marble pillars. An emperor in a purple robe sits on a throne looking stern and angry. SERGIUS and BACCHUS stand straight and calm before him, hands open, no fear, still in uniform. A palace attendant holds out two leather belts they have taken from them. Warm torchlight and cream stone, serious but not frightening.
4. A busy Roman marketplace in a Syrian city in daylight. SERGIUS and BACCHUS, now in plain undyed tunics without armor, with loose chains around their necks, walk side by side down the middle of the street with their heads held high. They are singing. Townspeople watch from stalls and doorways, some curious, some silent. A few guards walk ahead. Dignified, not cruel.
5. A dusty Roman road through the Syrian desert at golden hour. SERGIUS and BACCHUS in plain tunics walk in loose chains with two guards, followed by two loyal servants carrying bundles. Low rocky hills, a distant inn with a lamp. Their faces are tired but peaceful, as if singing. The sky glows orange and pink.
6. Courtyard of a Roman border fortress with sandstone walls beside a wide river, the Euphrates, in the distance. A commander named ANTIOCHUS, about 45, in a purple cloak and bronze armor, sits on a bench of judgment and speaks kindly but firmly. SERGIUS and BACCHUS stand before him in plain tunics, calm and steady. Guards stand at the sides, spears upright. Warm daylight.
7. A quiet stone prison cell at night with a small barred window showing stars. SERGIUS sits on a stone ledge, head resting on his hand, grieving. In the doorway stands BACCHUS, as a gentle, softly shining presence in a clean officer's tunic, smiling and comforting, with one hand reaching toward him. Painterly soft light only on BACCHUS, no beams, no blood, peaceful mood.
8. At dawn in the Syrian desert, outside the mudbrick walls of the small city of Resafa. A small group of ordinary Christian townspeople, men and women in simple robes, gather quietly around a simple grave marked by stones, with an oil lamp and a few wildflowers. A palm branch lies on the grave. Soft pink morning light, quiet and respectful. No violence, no body.
9. A grand early Byzantine church complex in the Syrian desert city of Resafa (Sergiopolis) with a large golden stone basilica and arches, at sunset. Pilgrims with walking staffs and camels arrive along a road; soldiers in bronze armor kneel in prayer at the entrance; banners and palm branches. Warm golden light, a sense of a crowd of many people coming from far away.
10. A modern-day scene: two children about 9 years old, one with brown skin and braids, one with red hair and glasses, sit side by side on school steps sharing a picture book and smiling. A third child sits alone nearby, looking over, and the two are waving them over to join. Morning sunlight, school bags, a friendly neighborhood. This is a modern scene.

Every image is an imaginative composite, not documentary. Pixel-checked against page text before acceptance.
