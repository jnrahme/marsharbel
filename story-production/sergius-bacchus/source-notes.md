# Saints Sergius and Bacchus storybook: research and editorial notes

Original retelling for ages 6-12. The only full account is the Passion of Sergius and Bacchus, probably written in the 5th century. The Catholic Encyclopedia says the martyrdom is "well authenticated" by early martyrologies and early veneration but calls the details "legend". So the script says "the old account" wherever it follows the Passion. Sources disagree on the emperor (the English Passion says Maximian; other summaries say Galerius), so the script names none and says "about the year 300" (Catholic Encyclopedia: about 303).
