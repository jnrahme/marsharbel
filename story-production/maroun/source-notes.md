# Saint Maroun storybook: research and editorial notes

Original retelling for ages 6-12. Maroun's life is thinly documented. Everything about his own life comes from one chapter by Theodoret of Cyrrhus (Religious History 16, plus mentions in chapters 6, 21, 22, 24 and 30). Nothing about his childhood or family is known, so the script has none. The page claims are:

| Page | Claim | Source | Confidence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Late 300s, hilltop near Cyrrhus, northern Syria; small tent seldom used; a place formerly honored by pagans dedicated to God | Theodoret, Religious History 16.1 (translation: https://www.fryuhanna.com/2026/01/28/maronite-history-st-theodoret-on-st-maroun/ ); dating 350-410 from Gorgias Encyclopedic Dictionary (https://gedsh.bethmardutho.org/Maron) | documented; date approximate |
| 2 | Admired the hermit Zebinas and sent visitors to him | Theodoret 24.2 (same page) | documented. Not called a "disciple": the text does not say it |
| 3 | Visitors came; healings reported; healed greed and anger | Theodoret 16.2-3 | documented by Theodoret, who did not meet Maroun |
| 4 | Self-control, justice, the garden image | Theodoret 16.3 | documented |
| 5 | Yaqub (James), Limnaios, Domnina; land of Cyrrhus a paradise | Theodoret 16.3, 21, 22, 30 | documented |
| 6 | Letter from John Chrysostom in exile, about 405, to "Maron, presbyter and monk" | https://romanletters.org/letters/chrysostom/36/ ; https://maronitefoundation.org/MaroniteFoundation/en/MaronitesHistory/67 | letter documented; identification with this Maroun is "many believe" |
| 7 | Short illness; death c. 410 (some say later); wish to be buried with Zebinas | Theodoret 16.4, 24.2 | documented; date approximate |
| 8 | Villages quarrelled; large village took him; church and yearly feast; "his memory" | Theodoret 16.4 | documented |
| 9 | Beth Maroun monastery near Apamea; Maronites; Lebanon; Charbel entered Monastery of St Maron, Annaya, 1851; Maroun's feast 9 February | https://gedsh.bethmardutho.org/Maron ; site page st-maroun.html | origins of the Maronite Church are debated, wording is hedged ("over the centuries") |
| 10 | Psalm 92:13 (NABRE numbering), used by Theodoret about Maroun's followers | https://bible.usccb.org/bible/psalms/92 | verified |

Miracles are reported as Theodoret's account, never promised. No invented quotes. The final page is an authored reflection, not a Maroun quotation.
