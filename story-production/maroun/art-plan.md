# Saint Maroun art plan

Style and negatives: see prompts below. Final art converted to 1280x720 WebP into media/storybook-maroun/images/.

**Palette:** Syrian limestone hills near Cyrrhus, late 4th century: pale gold stone, olive green, dusty ochre, clear morning blues.

**Characters:** MAROUN: a lean, weathered man of about 50 with sun-browned skin, short dark hair going gray and a trimmed gray beard, wearing a simple undyed wool tunic and a rough brown cloak, barefoot. Same face and clothes in every scene. A small tent made of dark goat-hair skins beside him whenever on the hilltop.

1. Dawn on a bare limestone hilltop in northern Syria, late 4th century. MAROUN stands facing the sky, hands open in quiet prayer, small dark goat-hair tent beside him, scattered old carved stones from a ruined pagan shrine at his feet. Wide open sky with soft apricot and blue clouds, rolling pale hills far below.
2. A rocky hillside path in warm afternoon light. MAROUN and a much older white-bearded hermit named ZEBINAS in a plain gray cloak stand together on the path. MAROUN gently gestures toward ZEBINAS, as if sending a visiting traveler to him; one traveler in a simple traveling cloak with a walking staff listens. Dry hills and olive trees behind.
3. Villagers climbing a stony hill path toward MAROUN, who stands at the top with one hand raised in blessing. A mother carries a sleepy sick toddler wrapped in a blanket, an older man leans on a staff, a young man helps him. Quiet, hopeful mood, bright morning light, goat-hair tent at the top, no glowing effects.
4. MAROUN sitting on a flat rock under an olive tree on a terraced slope, speaking with three different villagers who sit close and listen: a stern man with folded arms, a worried man holding a coin purse, a young man rubbing his eyes sleepily. Small green garden terraces with vines and flowers climb the slope behind them, as if a garden is blooming.
5. Sunset on a hilltop. MAROUN stands with three companions: a strong man named YAQUB in a brown cloak, another man named LIMNAIOS pointing toward a distant hilltop, and a woman named DOMNINA with a simple head veil and plain dress. Four goat-hair tents are visible on nearby hills. A feeling of a growing community, warm orange light.
6. A plain stone room in a small mountain town in 5th century Armenia, in winter light from one small window. An elderly bald bishop with a long white beard in a simple dark robe, JOHN CHRYSOSTOM, sits at a wooden table writing a letter on a scroll with a reed pen, a single oil lamp beside him. Through the window, snowy mountains. Quiet, tender mood.
7. Evening light inside a goat-hair tent on the hilltop. MAROUN lies resting on a simple woven mat with a rolled cloak as a pillow, calm and peaceful with eyes gently closed, covered by a plain blanket. His friend YAQUB sits beside him holding his hand, head bowed. Soft golden light through the tent opening, a view of the hills at dusk. Gentle, no drama.
8. Early morning on the hill road. People from a large village walk together in a quiet procession up the path carrying small oil lamps, some wearing head coverings, men carrying a simple wooden bier wrapped in white cloth (no body visible, fully covered). Smaller groups of people from other villages stand on the slope watching, wishing to have him near. Respectful, sorrowful, gentle mood. Pale gold light.
9. Sunrise over the Lebanese mountains with dark green cedar trees and an old stone monastery with a small bell tower. A young Maronite monk in a black hooded habit stands outdoors seen mostly from behind, looking up at the wide open sky, hands in prayer. A narrow stone path leads up the mountain behind him. Calm, hopeful mood, 19th century Lebanon.
10. A modern-day child about 9 years old with dark curly hair sits quietly on a smooth rock on a grassy hillside at sunrise, hands folded, eyes closed, in a simple t-shirt and jeans, a grandparent sitting beside them with a gentle smile. Distant mountains with cedar trees under a wide open sky. Peaceful, warm, hopeful. This is a modern scene.

Every image is an imaginative composite, not documentary. Pixel-checked against page text before acceptance.
