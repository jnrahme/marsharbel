# Francis of Assisi art plan (green-lit script)

Ten original 16:9 watercolor/gouache scenes; warm Assisi terracotta, olive hills, blue sky, painterly joyful childhood readability. Francis: Italian man, dark wavy hair, slim, increasingly plain brown habit; Clare distinct woman, not a copy of another book's saint. Historical composite where scene unknown. No lurid leprosy marks, war violence, animal grabbing, supernatural beams, copied famous paintings, lettering or speech balloons.

1. Youth in Assisi near cloth merchant's shop, imaginative scene.
2. Francis greeting a man with leprosy warmly, human dignity, no visible injury; safe distance/context.
3. San Damiano chapel repair, Francis carrying a modest stone with adult helper; call remains reported faith experience, not literal beam.
4. Adult renunciation conveyed by leaving fine cloth on table; clothed, no child imitation.
5. Friars walking in company through Umbrian landscape; no solo abandonment.
6. Francis, Clare and communities suggested in neighboring courtyards, no invented joint meeting.
7. Birds in trees as reported traditional motif and Creation praise; no bird handling.
8. Francis and Sultan in respectful seated encounter, no battle or dialogue.
9. Quiet later life with friars and nature; no death tableau.
10. Modern child and trusted adult caring for a garden safely; space for HTML reflection.

Pixel-check period progression, representations of illness and interfaith meeting, original artwork and child-safe posture; label composites.
