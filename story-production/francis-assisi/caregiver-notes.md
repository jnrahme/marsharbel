# Caregiver notes

Original art is imaginative, not documentary. The birds episode remains a reported early account, not a proven animal response. Children should not approach strangers, wild animals, war or hazardous travel to imitate the story. Helping with illness requires trusted adults and medical guidance; leprosy is treatable today. Francis's simple living was an adult choice, not permission to leave family or give away necessities. Ask trusted adults how to help safely.
