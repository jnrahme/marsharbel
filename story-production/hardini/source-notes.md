# Source notes and child-safety boundaries

Original child-suitable retelling, not copied historical dialogue. The hillside and classroom pictures, and the imagined bookbinding hands, are explicitly composites. The Church biography says Nimatullah taught and guided the scholasticate; the Holy See's Portuguese 1998 profile explicitly says Saint Charbel was formed in his school. We do not invent a recorded lesson or a literal student exchange. No healing miracle or promised outcome is asserted. Hard self-denial and lack of sleep are not modeled as a child's duty; rest, treatment, and help from trusted adults are named.
