# Saint Marina storybook - source notes

Status: tradition, not documented history. Secondary sources only; the primary vita text (Syriac/Arabic/Latin) was not accessed.

- Maronite Research Institute, Journal of Maronite Studies, July 2000 (Hourani): Qannoubine, father Eugenius, the accusation, raising the child, return, discovery at death, grotto tomb. http://www.maronite-institute.org/MARI/JMS/july00/Saint_Marina_The_Monk.htm
- Wikipedia "Marina the Monk" (citing Hourani 2013): ten years, age forty, sheep's milk, the monk blind in one eye. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marina_the_Monk
- OCA tells a different version (Bithynia/Alexandria, 6th c., three years outside, three back): https://www.oca.org/saints/lives/2026/02/12/100508-venerable-mary-who-was-called-marinus-and-her-father-venerable-e
- Douay-Rheims 1 Kings (1 Samuel) 16:7: https://www.drbo.org/chapter/09016.htm
- Site page: /saint-marina-qadisha (claim matrix 2026-09-30).
