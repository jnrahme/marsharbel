**Palette:** Lebanese mountain valley: pale limestone cliffs, deep cedar and pine green, olive, warm ochre, soft morning and golden-hour light.

**Era:** The story says only "the early centuries of Lebanese monastic life" (sources say 5th century or about 750). Clothing is plain, timeless Eastern-monastic and village wear: undyed wool tunics, dark wool monk habits with hoods. No armour, no crowns, no datable fashion.

**Characters:** MARINA as a child, about 11: olive skin, long dark brown braided hair, plain cream tunic and brown shawl. EUGENIUS: lean man about 45, short dark beard with some gray, plain brown wool robe. MARINOS (Marina as a monk): same face as Marina, hair cut short and covered by a dark hood, black-brown wool monk's habit with a rope belt, barefoot or in plain sandals. Same faces and clothes in every scene. THE ABBOT: elderly man, long white beard, dark habit, plain wooden cross on a cord. The CHILD (page 6-7): a baby, then a boy of about 7 in a plain tunic.

**Hard negatives (every prompt):** no text, letters or captions; no halos or glowing light effects; no modern objects in pages 1-8; no armour or weapons; no violence or fear; nothing sexual; no blood; no crowns; no Western European castle architecture.

2. Late afternoon on a hillside terrace. EUGENIUS gestures toward a faraway limestone cliff where a small stone monastery clings to the wall of a green valley (Qannoubine in the Qadisha Valley). MARINA, about 11, listens beside him with a quiet, thoughtful face. Cedars and pines in the valley.
3. Dusk at the stone doorway of a plain village house, one oil lamp. MARINA, about 12, looks up steadily at EUGENIUS and rests one hand gently on his arm as she asks her question; he looks back at her, surprised and moved. Warm lamp glow, no other people.
4. The arched stone gate of a cliffside mountain monastery in a deep green valley. EUGENIUS and MARINOS (short hair under a dark hood, dark monk's habit) stand side by side at the open gate; three kindly monks in dark habits welcome them with open hands. Morning light, cedars, a narrow path.
5. A plain stone room in the monastery. Elderly white-bearded ABBOT stands and points toward the door; MARINOS kneels calmly with head bowed, hands open, without defending herself; several monks stand quietly behind with troubled faces. Soft light from one small window. Gentle, no anger shown.
6. Dawn outside the monastery's stone gate. MARINOS in the dark habit sits on a flat stone beside the gate holding a small baby wrapped in a cloth; a shepherd in a simple tunic hands over a small clay bowl of milk; a few sheep nearby. Mountain cliff behind, soft pink light. Tender mood.
7. Late afternoon in the monastery courtyard. MARINOS, now clearly older and tired but calm, carries a large clay water jar on her shoulder; a boy of about 7 walks beside her carrying a small bundle; two monks at the gate hold it open for them. Warm light.
8. Evening inside a simple monastery chapel. Monks and the ABBOT stand and kneel around a plain wooden bier covered completely in a white cloth (no face or body shown); the ABBOT kneels with head bowed; monks hold oil lamps and wipe their eyes. Respectful and sorrowful, no drama.
