# Anthony of Padua art plan (green-lit script)

Ten original 16:9 painterly scenes in warm cream, amber, olive and lapis; period-inspired Portugal, Morocco and Italy, not documentary portraits. Fernando/Anthony: Portuguese man with dark curls, brown eyes, warm skin, young adult Augustinian dark robe first, then simple brown Franciscan habit, growing older subtly. No reuse of another saint's art. No miraculous apparitions, martyrdom or sickbed glamour, invented quotations, lettering, or unsafe solo travel. Symbolic scenes openly imaginative.

1. Young Fernando studying a book in Lisbon coastal town; composite.
2. Coimbra religious house library, young man studying Scripture.
3. Transition to Franciscan life: Anthony in brown habit meeting friars, with Morocco merely distant on a map-free horizon.
4. Morocco illness: Anthony as a young adult resting, cared for by adult companions, not alone or magically cured.
5. Quiet life near Forli; Anthony and companions in calm courtyard.
6. Teaching friars in Bologna, books and listening faces, no fabricated page text.
7. Preaching and respectful listening with ordinary neighbors, no crowd hysteria.
8. Late days near Padua, Anthony writing sermons in simple study, no death depiction.
9. Modern child listening kindly to teacher; clear present-day reflection.
10. Modern child and trusted adult seeking a lost object safely at home, open area for HTML reflection text. Never suggest searching alone for a missing person.
