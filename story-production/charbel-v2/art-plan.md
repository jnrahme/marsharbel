# Saint Charbel storybook v2: scene plan (new art style, matches series)

All images are imaginative painterly 16:9 scenes, never documentary images, and never copies of the historic photographs of Charbel. Keep the series' Levantine mountain palette of warm apricot, cream, olive, and blue; child-facing mood warm and welcoming without minimizing sorrow. Consistent hero: Youssef is a dark-haired Lebanese boy in simple 19th-century mountain village dress; young monk Charbel wears the black Maronite monastic habit with the monastic cap, dark beard; older Father Charbel has a long white beard and the same black habit. No stigmata imagery, no beams of literal heavenly light except the gentle, clearly-labeled lamp and tomb-light scenes, no text painted inside images.
