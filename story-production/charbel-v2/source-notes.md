# Saint Charbel storybook v2 - research and editorial notes

Original child-suitable retelling, not a copied transcript or eyewitness dialogue. Production source record; publishing is controlled by the site integrator, not this folder. The grotto, the lamp, and the mother's blessing are labeled as handed-down tradition, not documented public events. No character speech, miracle promise, or medical claim is invented. The illness and death scene is written gently and non-graphically; nothing frames suffering as desirable. Child-safety line: none needed beyond the reflection page's "who can you talk to" guidance.

All five source keys below are already wired into the player (`storybook.js` EVIDENCE_SOURCES), reused by v2. Their URLs and relevant claims were rechecked on September 29, 2026 before handoff.

- Vatican (Paul VI, canonization homily, Oct 9, 1977): https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/fr/homilies/1977/documents/hf_p-vi_hom_19771009.html . Supports the structured formation path: Mayfouk, Annaya, Kfifan, ordination 1859, 16 years of community life, 23 years of hermitage life.
- Vatican (Paul VI, beatification address, Dec 5, 1965): https://www.vatican.va/content/paul-vi/fr/speeches/1965/documents/hf_p-vi_spe_19651205_charbel-makhlouf.html . Supports early loss of his father and the beatification act and date.
- Monastery of Saint Maron, Annaya archive (Wayback capture): https://web.archive.org/web/20160304235515/http://www.saintcharbel-annaya.com/home.php?lgid=0 . Monastic tradition source: grotto devotion, water-lamp story, hermitage years, tomb lights, devotion after death.
- Biography cross-check: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Charbel_Makhlouf . Cross-check for dates: born May 8, 1828 Bekaa Kafra; entered Mayfouk 1851; name Charbel; solemn vows Nov 1, 1853; Kfifan studies under St Nimatullah al-Hardini; ordained July 23, 1859; hermitage of Saints Peter and Paul from Feb 15, 1875; illness during Mass Dec 16, 1898; died Dec 24, 1898; snowy burial; Nohad El Shami testimony 1993 (not retold in this book).
- Devotional tradition source: https://catholicsaints.info/saint-charbel-makhlouf/ . Corroborates the lamp tradition and devotional details; used only for items labeled tradition.

Reflection page (revised Oct 1): one question, one verse (Psalm 46:11, NABRE, verified at bible.usccb.org/bible/psalms/46: "Be still and know that I am God!"), one small action, one short prayer addressed to God. Authored pastoral application, not a quotation from Charbel. The mother's visit/blessing line was cut (no source found). The burial snow line reads "in the deep snow of that Christmas" (Wikipedia pallbearer quote: heavy snow). Nine narrative pages plus one narrated reflection page run 393.19 seconds (6:33.19 after the Oct 1 revision) in the final assembled MP3 files. Voice: Kokoro am_michael at speed .94, with 0.27-second sentence-end rests and 0.11-second internal fragment rests. Audio read-through was checked against the script via transcript inspection. The transcription of the first page rendered Bekaa Kafra as Bikfaya, an automatic speech-to-text mismatch; pronunciation alternatives were tested but not adopted because they made the pronunciation worse. The page text is correct.

Audio check (Oct 1): all ten pages transcribed with two engines (faster-whisper tiny.en and small.en) and diffed against the script. Pages 6, 8, 10 matched near-exactly; remaining diffs were proper-noun spellings (Annaya heard as Ania/Anaya, Kfifan, Mayfouk, Youssef). Bekaa Kafra was heard as Bikacafra; fixed with a Kokoro phoneme override (/bɛkˈɑː/ /kˈɑːfɹə/) and re-rendered pages 1-2, now heard as Bekah Kafra/Bikka Kafra. Not a human ear-grade listen.
