    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who was Saint Anthony of Padua?

    Born Fernando in Lisbon around 1195, Anthony became a Franciscan friar, preacher and teacher. He died near Padua in 1231.

    Who is this storybook for?

    Children ages 6 to 12, reading or listening with a trusted adult. The final page has a question, Bible reading, safe action and prayer.

    Does prayer guarantee finding a lost thing?

    No. Asking Anthony to pray is a devotional practice, not a promise. Practical help matters; a missing person needs immediate adult and trained help.
