    Read the Bible and pray together

    With a trusted adult, read Matthew 5:9. Jesus calls peacemakers blessed. Talk about one small act of peace: listening, including someone or asking a trusted adult for help when someone is hurt.

    About this story

    Illustrations are imaginative composites, not historical records. The chapel repair scene does not literally depict the reported call. The clothed cloth-table scene symbolizes renunciation, rather than reconstructing the historical gesture. Neighboring courtyards suggest the friars and Clare's community, not a documented joint meeting or the approval in Rome. The bird scene illustrates an attributed early account, not a verified animal response.

    Children should not approach strangers, wild animals, war or hazardous travel to imitate this story. Help involving illness needs trusted adults and medical guidance; leprosy is treatable today. Francis's simple living was an adult choice, not permission to leave family or give away necessities. Ask a trusted adult how to help safely.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Who was Saint Francis of Assisi?

    Francis was born in Assisi around 1182. He founded the Friars Minor and is remembered for simple living, care for neighbors, prayer and praise for creation.

    Who is this storybook for?

    Children ages 6 to 12, reading or listening with a trusted adult. The final page has a question, Bible reading, safe action and prayer.

    Are the pictures exact historical scenes?

    No. The sourced events and reported accounts are distinguished in the page notes. The pictures are imaginative composites, not eyewitness records.
